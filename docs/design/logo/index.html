How these were made

Each mark is a simple shape drawn in code, in the site's navy and gold. The pointed roof in L1 comes from the Dewan Rakyat chamber building in Kuala Lumpur. The curved rows in L2 follow the chamber's seating. The diamond is the songket motif the site already uses.

The mark you pick goes into the site header, the browser-tab icon, the phone icon and the share image. I can still tune its details after you pick.
