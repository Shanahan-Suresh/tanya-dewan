Seven upgrades that show the engineering

The site works, but it looks like a plain records site. These seven upgrades put the search, the data and the build on screen. Each one below works with real data from the site.

  1. 1Try each upgrade in its frame.
  2. 2Press Build it, Maybe or Skip under it.
  3. 3Tell me in chat when you are done.
0 of 7 picked
1

Watch it search

The answer screen shows each search step as it happens. Sources appear as soon as they are ranked, before the answer text.

tanya-dewan/?q=Can+my+employer+cut+my+salary
What a visitor sees
The search narrow 428,000 records down to the 3 it cites, with the real times. A switch shows the raw stream from the server.
What it shows a hiring manager
Server-sent events, a hybrid search pipeline and care for how fast an answer feels.
What it takes
Front end, plus a small server change: send the sources when ranking ends, and each passage's rank at each step.
Works in the free demo?
Yes, after one new recording
2

Citation reader

Each number in the answer opens the whole Act section beside it, with the quoted words marked.

tanya-dewan/?q=Can+my+employer+cut+my+salary#source-1
What a visitor sees
Press a number, and the source opens beside the answer. The quoted words are marked. Use J and K to step through the sources and Esc to close.
What it shows a hiring manager
Answers you can check, focus control and keyboard access. On a phone the reader is a bottom sheet.
What it takes
Front end only. The full passage text is already in the answer the server sends.
Works in the free demo?
Yes
3

Jump to anything

Press Ctrl K anywhere to find an MP, Act, bill, topic or sitting by typing a few letters.

tanya-dewan/
What a visitor sees
A search box that finds any of 1,611 pages as they type, grouped by kind, with the matched letters marked.
What it shows a hiring manager
A search endpoint, fuzzy matching and an accessible list you can drive with the arrow keys.
What it takes
A /api/suggest endpoint on the SQLite full-text index for the live site, and a prebuilt list for the demo.
Works in the free demo?
Yes, from a prebuilt list
4

The Dewan seat map

All 222 seats of the 15th Parliament in one half circle. It lights up one MP's seat, or everyone who spoke in a debate.

tanya-dewan/mp/dzulkefly-ahmad
What a visitor sees
The house at a glance. Point at a seat to see who holds it. It can be the home page picture, and it fits on MP and debate pages.
What it shows a hiring manager
A custom chart drawn from real records, with no chart library.
What it takes
One query for the seat and party of each MP. The seat order is a diagram, not the real seating plan, and the page says so.
Works in the free demo?
Yes
5

Debate as a timeline

A strip at the top of a debate page shows every speech in order, sized by length and coloured by party.

tanya-dewan/debate/DR-2024-12-11-040
What a visitor sees
Who spoke, how long and when, before reading a word. Press a bar, or use the arrow keys, to read that speech.
What it shows a hiring manager
Messy PDF transcripts turned into clean, structured data that a chart can trust.
What it takes
Front end only. Speaker, party, time and length are already in the database.
Works in the free demo?
Yes
6

Topic explorer

How often Parliament debated each big topic, month by month since July 2018, with key events marked.

tanya-dewan/topics
What a visitor sees
Peaks they can explain: the budget every October and November, and the gap at the first lockdown. Drag across the chart to zoom in.
What it shows a hiring manager
An interactive chart over 30,091 debates, with zoom, hover and topic switches.
What it takes
Monthly counts per topic, built once into a small JSON file.
Works in the free demo?
Yes

How it's built, plus polish

A page that maps the whole system. Press a box to see what it does, its real numbers and its code.

tanya-dewan/how-it-works
What a visitor sees
The path from PDF to answer, with the test results. Below it are three small touches: a light and dark switch, smooth page changes and a ? list of keys.
What it shows a hiring manager
That you can design and measure a whole system. Recruiters often read this page first.
What it takes
Front end only. The numbers come from the README and the eval reports.
Works in the free demo?
Yes
