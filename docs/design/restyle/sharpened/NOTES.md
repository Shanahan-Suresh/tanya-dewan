# Sharpened

Today's identity, tightened to a 2026 studio finish: the same warm ground, navy and yellow, Geist and Literata, songket diamonds, but on a 12-column grid with large tight display type, one radius scale and layered shadows instead of flat borders. The ask box is the hero, set inside the 222-seat chamber drawn as fine gold dots.

- Type: Geist 400 to 700 for the interface, display at clamp(2.5rem, 6.4vw, 5.2rem) with -0.045em tracking; Literata for verbatim record text and quotes.
- Colour: ground #FAF7F0, ink #0E1A3C, navy #0A1F5C as the primary button, yellow #F5B800 for the underline, the cited seats and the primary button in dark mode. Six coalition colours, re-tuned for dark.
- Radius 8, 14, 22. Three shadow levels. Songket texture at 4 to 8 per cent in the hero only.
- Modern touches: header condenses on scroll; numbers count up once; sections reveal once; cards lift on hover; the chamber lights stage by stage while an answer runs, then three seats stay lit for the three citations; funnel as a slim six-column chart; answer left, sources right; topic charts draw once and read out a year on hover.
- Reveal, count-up and draw animations switch off for automated browsers and reduced-motion users, so screenshots show the final state.
- Hard to build on the real site: the chamber is a computed layout of 222 SVG circles (needs a server-side seat list and a small script); the stage-by-stage lighting depends on the stage events already in the stream; sticky condensing header and scroll reveals need the observer script on every page.
