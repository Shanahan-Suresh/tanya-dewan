Summary (AI)

What happened

Datuk Seri Dr. Ronald Kiandee [Beluran] asked whether the government would review the existing policy of 70 per cent local and 30 per cent imported rice to keep the padi industry sustainable under DAN 2.0. Dato’ Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim replied that planting would be expanded and that the RM1 billion approved for MADA would be launched at the end of the month to raise local supply from about 56 to 70 per cent. Datuk Seri Dr. Ronald Kiandee then raised rising production costs, higher seed prices, diesel subsidies under the SKDS scheme not covering some machinery, and rice no longer selling at RM2.60 per kilogram, and asked whether the government was sympathetic to farmers’ difficulties and their request for a RM1,800 per tonne floor price. Dato’ Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim answered by listing aid given since 2023, including RM228 million added to the Bantuan Khas Sumbangan Tunai, RM60 million in BERNAS special aid, and the Skim Takaful Tanaman Padi, and said the Minister of Agriculture would table a paper next week. He also said the government would bear about RM150 million for six months so that consumer prices of local rice would not rise. Dr. Mohammed Taufiq bin Johari [Sungai Petani] and Tuan Hassan bin Abdul Karim [Pasir Gudang] stood up but said nothing of substance.
