Questions answered

Matched and summarised by AI. The linked speeches are the record.

Question (AI)

What are the latest developments on alleged 'mafia korporat' activity involving breaches of the law, and what action has been taken?

Asked by Dato’ Seri Utama Haji Aminuddin bin Harun [Port Dickson]

Answer (AI)

The PM said corporate crimes such as takeovers of listed companies, share manipulation, disclosure breaches and illegal moneylending are being tackled, with enforcement stepped up by the Securities Commission, Bank Negara, SPRM and PDRM/AMLA, and that cases would not be tolerated.

Answered by Perdana Menteri dan Menteri Kewangan [Dato’ Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim]

Question (AI)

How will the SC, Bank Negara and PDRM widen investigations into cross-border corporate crime, how will cooperation with foreign bodies work, and are law amendments needed?

Asked by Dato’ Seri Utama Haji Aminuddin bin Harun [Port Dickson]

Answer (AI)

Proposed amendments so far only cover government-linked companies and agencies, with the State-Owned Enterprises Act to be tabled by year end or early next year; existing laws are considered sufficient but political resolve is needed; Bank Negara was recognised in the 2025 FATF mutual evaluation.

Answered by Perdana Menteri dan Menteri Kewangan [Dato’ Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim]

Question (AI)

How far have investigations into alleged corporate mafia gone, and was the alleged RM9.5 million payment to a minister sakau, a donation, a gift or cekau?

Asked by Dato’ Rosol bin Wahid [Hulu Terengganu]

Answer (AI)

Investigations continue when there are charges and the Prime Minister does not pre-judge; the RM9.5 million claim is a political statement with no evidence, and the minister has challenged it and taken the matter to court.

Answered by Dato’ Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim
