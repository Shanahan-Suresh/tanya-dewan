15 October 2026Asked by Dr. Hajah Halimah Ali [Kapar]The debate
Question AI summary

Has the government identified hospitals in high-risk haze areas and proactively placed suitable specialists, especially lung specialists, there?

Answer AI summary

The minister said haze-related disease surveillance is done through sentinel health facilities, with hotspots monitored more closely, air purifiers added, and risks stratified by area, under the Director-General of Health.

15 October 2026Asked by Tuan Cha Kee Chin [Rasah]The debate
Question AI summary

Should schools close and outdoor activities be restricted at certain IPU levels, and does KKM give advice on this?

Answer AI summary

KKM advises limiting outdoor activities above IPU 100 and advises closing schools, kindergartens and nurseries above IPU 200; closure decisions rest with the KPM and KPT ministers under the National Haze Action Plan.

15 October 2026Asked by Tuan Cha Kee Chin [Rasah]The debate
Question AI summary

What actions and initiatives does the ministry take to address the public health impact of haze, including cooperation with JAS and agencies?

Answer AI summary

The ministry runs year-round haze-related illness surveillance at 78 sentinel facilities, reports rising URTI, asthma and conjunctivitis cases, adds medical supplies, and cooperates with JAS, METMalaysia, NADMA, KPM and KBS, and advises the public to monitor IPU.

12 October 2026Asked by Dato' Dr. Hj. Alias bin Razak [Kuala Nerus]The debate
Question AI summary

What is the status of lean management at Hospital Tengku Ampuan Rahimah, and will the model be extended to other hospitals, including in Terengganu?

Answer AI summary

Lean management is a pilot project that has been applied in various initiatives across hospitals, with a management efficiency journey from registration to discharge; stabilised patients are stepped down to district hospitals to reduce bed occupancy rate and waiting times.
