After being called by Deputy Speaker Puan Alice Lau Kiong Yieng, Tuan Cha Kee Chin (Rasah) asked the Works Minister for the current status, progress percentage, expected completion date and causes of delay for the FT362 upgrade from Simpang Tol Bandar Ainsdale to Seremban town. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan, Deputy Minister of Works, replied that completion is scheduled for 17 May 2029, progress is 7.62 per cent against a scheduled 7.62 per cent, and land acquisition for 18 of 46 private lots remains, expected to finish by the end of November 2026. Tuan Cha Kee Chin then asked for the results and preliminary findings of the Jalan Sungai Ujong traffic study, including the two-week closure of a traffic light junction after the Seremban toll plaza exit, whether it would be fully implemented, and when results would be announced. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan replied that the junction is 155 metres from the PLUS junction against a 300-metre guideline, that the closure eased traffic but the U-turn at Jalan Labu FT362 is small and narrow so a long-term upgrade is proposed, and that congestion persisted because road users made unauthorised U-turns at Palm Mall, so JKR could not yet reach a conclusive finding, and he referred the Rasah MP to JKR Seremban. Dato’ Sri Dr. Haji Ismail bin Abd Muttalib (Maran) asked how many contractors had been penalised, including contract termination, what losses the government had incurred, and the status of the FT83 Jengka 8 to Pusat Jengka upgrade. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan replied that 70 to 75 per cent of the troubled projects among about 850 are caused by contractors, that the loss has not yet been assessed, and that the FT83 question will be answered in writing, while Tuan Zakri bin Hassan (Kangar) stood to ask question 11 with no substantive content.

Ringkasan dalam Bahasa Melayu

Selepas dijemput oleh Timbalan Yang di-Pertua Puan Alice Lau Kiong Yieng, Tuan Cha Kee Chin (Rasah) meminta Menteri Kerja Raya menyatakan status terkini, peratus kemajuan, tarikh dijangka siap dan punca kelewatan bagi projek menaik taraf Jalan FT362 dari Simpang Tol Bandar Ainsdale ke bandar Seremban. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan, Timbalan Menteri Kerja Raya, menjawab bahawa projek dijadual siap pada 17 Mei 2029, kemajuan ialah 7.62 peratus berbanding jadual 7.62 peratus, dan pengambilan balik tanah bagi 18 daripada 46 lot persendirian masih berbaki, dijangka selesai pada penghujung November 2026. Tuan Cha Kee Chin kemudian bertanya tentang status dan dapatan awal kajian penyuraian trafik di Jalan Sungai Ujong, termasuk penutupan simpang lampu isyarat selama dua minggu selepas exit Plaza Tol Seremban, sama ada ia akan dilaksanakan sepenuhnya, dan bila keputusan akan diumumkan. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan menjawab bahawa persimpangan itu berjarak 155 meter dari persimpangan PLUS berbanding garis panduan 300 meter, penutupan itu melancarkan trafik tetapi U-turn di Jalan Labu FT362 kecil dan sempit sehingga naik taraf jangka panjang dicadangkan, dan kesesakan berterusan kerana pengguna jalan raya membuat U-turn tidak sah di Palm Mall, justeru JKR belum dapat membuat kesimpulan konklusif; beliau merujuk Ahli Parlimen Rasah kepada JKR Seremban. Dato’ Sri Dr. Haji Ismail bin Abd Muttalib (Maran) bertanya berapa ramai kontraktor telah dikenakan tindakan termasuk penamatan kontrak, berapa kerugian kerajaan, dan status menaik taraf Jalan Persekutuan FT83 Jengka 8 ke Pusat Jengka. Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan menjawab bahawa 70 hingga 75 peratus projek sakit daripada kira-kira 850 projek disebabkan kontraktor, kerugian belum ditaksir, dan soalan FT83 akan dijawab secara bertulis, manakala Tuan Zakri bin Hassan (Kangar) bangun untuk soalan 11 tanpa kandungan substantif.

Puan Yang di-Pertua, pertanyaan yang dikemukakan oleh Yang Berhormat Rasah adalah merujuk kepada projek menaik taraf Jalan FT362 dari Seksyen 0.4 ke Seksyen 3.1, Lingkaran Tengah Seremban dan membina jejambat merentasi Lebuhraya Utara Selatan, daerah Seremban, Negeri Sembilan. Skop projek ini ialah menaik taraf jalan tunggal dua lorong kepada jalan kembar empat lorong dua hala sepanjang 2.7 kilometer termasuk membina jambatan dengan piawaian JKR U5, menaik taraf persimpangan sedia ada, pemasangan lampu jalan dan lampu isyarat, menaik taraf sistem perparitan, kerja-kerja geoteknik, kerja- kerja pavement, pemasangan perabot jalan, pengambilan balik tanah dan pengalihan utiliti. Status kemajuan projek sehingga 30 September 2026 telah mencapai 7.62 peratus berbanding jadual juga sebanyak 7.62 peratus, iaitu 100 peratus ikut jadual. Keseluruhan proses pengambilan balik tanah ini dijangka dapat diselesaikan sepenuhnya pada penghujung November 2026. Pada masa ini, pihak kontraktor sedang melaksanakan kerja-kerja di tapak yang melibatkan sebahagian lot yang telah selesai pelaksanaan proses pengambilan balik tanah dan di kawasan tanah kerajaan. Pihak kontraktor juga giat melaksanakan kerja-kerja fizikal yang melibatkan kerja-kerja pembinaan longkang di tepi jalan di lokasi tertentu. Penyelarasan bersama syarikat-syarikat utiliti yang terlibat dan agensi kerajaan yang berkaitan sedang dilaksanakan bagi memastikan isu pengalihan utiliti dapat diselesaikan dengan segera. Pihak JKR akan memantau secara rapi bagi memastikan pihak kontraktor mematuhi spesifikasi yang ditetapkan, kawalan kos dan kualiti keseluruhan projek selaras dengan Arahan Teknik Jalan yang berkuat kuasa. Kementerian Kerja Raya melalui JKR selaku agensi pelaksana akan terus memantau kemajuan projek ini secara berkala bagi memastikan ia dilaksanakan mengikut jadual, berjaya disiapkan mengikut perancangan. Bacaan Kali Yang Kedua dan Ketiga

Bacaan Kali Yang Kedua dan Ketiga

Datuk Haji Awang bin Hashim moved the second and third reading of the Rang Undang-undang Kerja (Pindaan) 2021, stating that the Employment Act 1955 (Act 265) sets minimum terms for private-sector workers and that the amendments aim to strengthen worker protection and align with ILO standards. He said the bill followed consultations since 2015, was first read on 25 October 2021, and contains 46 clauses: 28 amended, 10 new and six repealed. He outlined several amendments, including extending the written appeal period to the Director General of Labour from 14 to 21 days, extending maternity leave from 60 to 90 days, reducing working hours from 48 to 45 hours a week, and introducing three days of paternity leave for married male workers. His speech ended while he was still describing clause 44 on the presumption of who is an employee and employer.

Bacaan Kali Yang Kedua dan Ketiga

Datuk Haji Awang bin Hashim, continuing his speech on the Rang Undang-undang Kerja (Pindaan) 2021, said the bill would insert a new section 101C into Akta 265 on presumptions about who is an employee and employer, remove Bahagian VIII on women working at night and underground under Fasal 10, remove section 60L under Fasal 26 and section 93 under Fasal 42, and remove the wage-limit clauses under Fasal 15, 29 and 35 so the Act would cover all private-sector workers, with the Jadual Pertama amended by an order to be enforced on a date yet to be decided. He also said the Jabatan Tenaga Kerja Semenanjung Malaysia had 580 officers, a ratio of one officer to 24,207 workers, and hoped the number would rise toward the International Labor Organization's suggested 1:20,000 ratio. Timbalan Yang di-Pertua Dato' Mohd Rashid Hasnon asked for a seconder, and Timbalan Menteri Pembangunan Usahawan dan Koperasi Datuk Muslimin bin Yahaya seconded the motion. The Deputy Speaker then opened the second reading debate, read out a list of 21 speakers and began with Ahmad Fadhli bin Shaari (Pasir Mas), who started his speech. M. Kulasegaran (Ipoh Barat) raised a point of order asking for 15 or 20 minutes instead of five, and Sivarasa Rasiah (Sungai Buloh) said the House's scheduling should be better arranged and that hurried passage of important amendments was improper, to which the Deputy Speaker replied that another bill was to be debated that day and they would proceed first.
