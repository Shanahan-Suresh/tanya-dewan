Q (AI)

Why are road deaths still high despite amended laws and stronger enforcement, and what is the measurable 2030 target and progress so far?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Pang Hok Liong [Labis]

A (AI)

The ministry says 6,581 road deaths were recorded in 2025, 66 per cent involving motorcyclists, and it is focusing on speeding, overloaded commercial vehicles, motorcyclists and hotspots, expanding AWAS, ANPR, speed limitation devices, telematics and HS-WIM, running MyLesen B2 and MyB2S, and shifting PKJRM phase two to measure outcomes rather than operations or summonses.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Pengangkutan [Tuan Loke Siew Fook]

Angka yang diberi (6)
  • 6,581 road deaths recorded in 2025
  • 4,542 deaths involving motorcycle riders and pillion riders
  • 66 peratus share of road deaths involving motorcyclists
  • RM30 juta allocation for MyLesen B2 and MyB2S in 2026
  • 80,648 participants planned for MyLesen B2 and MyB2S in 2026
  • 70,000 SIRIM-standard helmets distributed since 2025

Q (AI)

When will the government introduce rules requiring insurers to control or reduce health insurance premiums?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Pang Hok Liong [Labis]

A (AI)

The government understands public concern; the 2024 MHIT premium rise was due to a sharp rise in claims after COVID and delayed premium adjustments; Bank Negara has introduced interim measures since December 2024; a sustainable solution must address root causes of rising healthcare costs, not only premiums.

  • lebih daripada 23,000 Number of affected policies reactivated as of December 2025

Q (AI)

What are the main advantages of MediAsas over existing health insurance plans in coverage, premium affordability and benefits?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Pang Hok Liong [Labis]

A (AI)

MediAsas will offer more affordable coverage by setting criteria for hospitals, with Tier 1 and Tier 2 hospitals, requiring transparency on price and staged DRG implementation to control costs at Tier 1 hospitals so premiums can be reduced or stabilised over the long term.

How many Palestinian students are studying at public universities, and what total fees and costs did the government bear?
