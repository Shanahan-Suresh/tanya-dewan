What penalties apply to LLPs and individuals who fail to comply with beneficial ownership reporting?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Armizan bin Mohd Ali

A (AI)

An LLP must submit beneficial ownership information within 14 days and can be fined up to RM20,000 for failing to store or report it. An individual who fails to inform the LLP of their beneficial ownership status can be fined up to RM50,000 or jailed up to three years.
