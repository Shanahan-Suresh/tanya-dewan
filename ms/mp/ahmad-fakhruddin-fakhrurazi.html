Q (AI)

Will interim operating permits be given to Class B1 boats while black boxes are repaired, and will the three-month limit on the skipper's assistant letter be removed?

Ditanya oleh Dr. Ahmad Fakhruddin bin Fakhrurazi [Kuala Kedah]

A (AI)

Interim relief cannot be given for now because the AIS or MTU units are a licence renewal requirement. DOF has listed eight MTU suppliers so owners can choose one, and a hotline will be set up. Removing the three-month limit is not applicable because SOP since 29 June 2015 extended the approval period to a maximum of six months.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan [Datuk Chan Foong Hin]

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • lapan number of MTU equipment suppliers listed by DOF
  • enam bulan maximum approval period for skipper's assistant under the SOP
  • 30 hari previous approval period before the SOP change
  • 29 Jun 2015 date the SOP extension took effect
  • tiga bulan validity period of the approval that the MP asked to remove

Q (AI)

What facilities and service improvements are offered to fishermen to raise productivity, and how does KPKM intervene to speed vessel licence registration and approval?

Ditanya oleh Dr. Ahmad Fakhruddin bin Fakhrurazi [Kuala Kedah]

A (AI)

A stakeholder engagement session with Kuala Kedah fishermen will be held on 15 October 2026. Licensing is being digitalised to speed up document checks and reduce bureaucracy. Dijawab oleh Datuk Chan Foong Hin, Timbalan Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan

  • 1983 year the freeze on new Zone A, B and C licences began
  • 16 hingga 48 peratus estimated share of original biomass remaining for demersal and pelagic stocks
  • tiga tahun period of inactivity after which a licence is reviewed for cancellation
