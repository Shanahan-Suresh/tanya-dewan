Q (AI)

What is the main justification for the new MyKad, and how far do its security features, chip technology and verification system curb forgery, cloning and misuse while protecting personal data?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohamad Shafizan Haji Kepli [Batang Lupar]

A (AI)

The new MyKad replaces the card introduced more than 20 years ago and has 53 security features against 23 on the existing card; personal data is protected under an ISMS that complies with ISO/IEC7001.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Dalam Negeri [Datuk Seri Dr. Shamsul Anuar bin Haji Nasarah]

Angka yang diberi (4)
  • lebih 20 tahun lalu time since the existing MyKad structure was introduced
  • 53 security features on the new MyKad
  • 23 security features on the existing card
  • ISO/IEC7001 standard the ISMS complies with

Q (AI)

What cross-agency plans exist to tackle lending of MyKad to foreigners, and can existing law arrest and prosecute both parties and what penalties apply?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohamad Shafizan Haji Kepli [Batang Lupar]

A (AI)

Enforcement by JPN, the Immigration Department and JPK under the Ministry of Human Resources has been carried out; offences under Peraturan 25(1)(e) and 25(1)(n) apply, with a maximum penalty of three years' imprisonment or a RM20,000 fine, or both.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Seri Dr. Shamsul Anuar bin Haji Nasarah, Timbalan Menteri Dalam Negeri

Angka yang diberi (7)
  • 2022 hingga September 2026 period covered by the enforcement figures
  • 4,174 enforcement operations carried out
  • 1,868 arrests and prosecutions recorded
  • 1,191 offences recorded under the Peraturan-peraturan Pendaftaran Negara 1990
  • 669 offences recorded under Akta Kelahiran dan Kematian [Akta 255]
  • tidak melebihi tiga tahun maximum prison term for the offence
  • RM20,000 maximum fine for the offence

Q (AI)

Has a socioeconomic impact assessment been done for PROSPEK Sebuyau, and what were its main findings?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohamad Shafizan Haji Kepli [Batang Lupar]

A (AI)

An impact study was done first, with data collected by a UNIMAS team led by Prof. Dr. Affendy Arip. Q (AI)

What criteria and factors does JKM consider when reassessing eligibility and tightening monthly aid for existing recipients?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohamad Shafizan Haji Kepli [Batang Lupar]

A (AI)

JKM uses citizenship, residence and the 2024 poverty line income as conditions. Existing recipients are reviewed each year two months before aid ends, and aid continues for one year if they remain eligible. The 2026 allocation rose from the 2025 allocation.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Pembangunan Wanita, Keluarga dan Masyarakat [Puan Lim Hui Ying], Timbalan Menteri Pembangunan Wanita, Keluarga dan Masyarakat

Angka yang diberi (11)
  • 11 number of types of financial aid provided by JKM
  • RM1,236 2024 poverty line income (PGK) rate
  • RM1,198 previous 2022 food PGK rate
  • RM348 2024 per capita PGK rate
  • RM322 2022 per capita PGK rate
  • 1 Januari 2026 date JKM began using the 2024 PGK as a condition
  • dua bulan time before aid ends when existing recipients are reviewed
  • satu tahun period for which aid continues if recipients remain eligible
  • RM2.9 bilion amount allocated to the ministry through JKM for 2026
  • RM2.7 bilion amount allocated for 2025
  • 7.41 peratus increase in allocation from 2025 to 2026

Q (AI)

Can ineligible recipients be told earlier, and is there an appeal or support mechanism for those affected by terminated aid?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohamad Shafizan Haji Kepli [Batang Lupar]

A (AI)

Recipients must submit a review form two months before aid ends. Terminated applicants may reapply, with JKM officers investigating and assessing eligibility. Angka yang diberi (3)
  • dua bulan time before aid ends when the review form must be submitted

  • JKM18 application form for reapplying after termination
