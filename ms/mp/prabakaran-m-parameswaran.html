Q (AI)

What is the current status of the land and flood retention pond capacity in Batu and Jinjang, and how much area remains fully functional compared with the original?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Prabakaran a/l M Parameswaran [Batu]

There are 28 retention ponds overseen by DBKL and JPS. 19 lots were alienated to developers since 2015, reducing the area compared with the original 3.5 hectares. Q (AI)

What is the justification for accelerating the gazettement of the land, and what method is used?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Prabakaran a/l M Parameswaran [Batu]

A (AI)

Gazettement is being accelerated so that flood retention land is not alienated again. MACC investigated and found no case because the reservation was approved in 1998. On 9 October 2016 the committee agreed to cancel that approval to allow 80 acres to be alienated to a developer for a RM214 million premium. Two taskforces were set up, and 88 open-space lots were gazetted in eight months.

Dijawab oleh Puan Hannah Yeoh Tseow Suan, Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Wilayah Persekutuan)

  • 19 Jun 1998 date the reservation approval was granted

  • 80 ekar area proposed for alienation to the developer
  • RM214 juta premium payable for the mixed development
  • 18 tahun delay in starting gazettement of the area
  • lapan bulan period in which 88 lots were gazetted
  • 88 lot number of open-space lots gazetted

Q (AI)

Whether public roads built on government land may be sold or handed to the private sector, and what mechanisms keep public access open.

Ditanya oleh Tuan Prabakaran a/l M Parameswaran [Batu]

A (AI)

The government does not allow public roads on government land to be sold or handed to the private sector, especially main roads. Private involvement is limited to management or maintenance under concession agreements, and closure, disposal or status change requires gazettement and approval under the named Acts.
