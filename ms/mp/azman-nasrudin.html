Q (AI)

What social impact does rapid development and urbanisation in the Federal Territories have on local communities, especially social competition and rising living costs?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Azman bin Nasrudin [Padang Serai]

A (AI)

Pressures are greatest in Kuala Lumpur. Competition for housing, jobs, public facilities and quality urban space may widen social gaps and raise rent and transport costs. The minister cited earlier answers and plans, including PSKL 2040, PTKL 2040, Residensi MADANI, RE_NEW and CHASE City, as the approach to these impacts.

Dijawab oleh Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Wilayah Persekutuan) [Datuk Seri Dr. Zaliha binti Mustafa]

  • 2031 year Kuala Lumpur is projected to reach its peak population
  • 28 tahun years earlier than the national average that Kuala Lumpur reaches its population peak
  • 20 meter persegi target green and open space per resident in the capital
  • 2.35 juta projected Kuala Lumpur population by 2040 under PSKL 2040
  • 762,500 housing units needed in Kuala Lumpur by 2040
  • 40 peratus share of new housing targeted as affordable housing for B40 and part of M40
