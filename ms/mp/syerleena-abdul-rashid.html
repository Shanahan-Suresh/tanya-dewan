What procedures apply to abused or neglected children needing urgent protection, and what are the requirements for families wanting to foster or adopt children who have been abandoned?

Ditanya oleh Puan Syerleena binti Abdul Rashid [Bukit Bendera]

A (AI)

Complaints can come through Talian Kasih, hospitals, the police and others; a protection officer gazetted under Act 611 investigates, and the court decides on temporary or permanent placement, with periodic monitoring. Q (AI)

What is the government's proposal to regulate age verification for social media platforms, given the transfer of citizens' personal data to foreign platforms?

Ditanya oleh Puan Syerleena binti Abdul Rashid [Bukit Bendera]

A (AI)

MCMC is evaluating and testing age verification with providers through a regulatory sandbox. Act 866 took effect on 1 January 2026, with other subsidiary instruments due in the second quarter of 2026. After evaluation, the duty to keep users aged 16 and below off social media will be placed on providers, and licensed providers may face penalties of up to RM10 million.

Dijawab oleh Puan Teo Nie Ching, Timbalan Menteri Komunikasi

  • suku kedua 2026 expected timing for other subsidiary instruments to take effect

  • RM10 juta maximum financial penalty for licensed providers for non-compliance

Q (AI)

What existing mechanisms and enforcement does MCMC have against AI misuse such as non-consensual deepfakes of adults, and what is its long-term strategy against new AI platforms lacking safety guidelines?

Ditanya oleh Puan Syerleena binti Abdul Rashid [Bukit Bendera]

A (AI)

Options are limited beyond reactive action. Grok did not have a nudify function at launch and added it only late last year, but after complaints from several governments it announced removal. Malaysia has clear laws: under ONSA, CSAM and adult harmful content must be taken down immediately by licensed platforms, and CMA section 233 makes sharing obscene or indecent content an offence. Developers should follow guardrails and safety by design, and clear laws and immediate action by all governments are important.

Dijawab oleh Puan Teo Nie Ching, Timbalan Menteri Komunikasi

Angka yang diberi (1)
  • seksyen 233 section of the Communications and Multimedia Act making it an offence to share obscene, indecent or offensive content

What is the purpose and benefit of the Malaysia Digital Inclusivity Index for socioeconomic development?
