Q (AI)

What has the ministry achieved in strengthening digital literacy and AI skills across sectors and rural areas, and what is its plan for high-skilled digital talent?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Tan Hong Pin [Bakri]

A (AI)

The ministry follows a whole-of-nation approach with access, awareness and adoption pillars, runs the digital learning portal, AI programmes for youth, Coursera licences, rural outreach, the Executive Digital Leadership Programme, Gemini Workspace access for civil servants, and is developing a national digital talent framework with AI Malaysia, TalentCorp and KESUMA.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Digital [Tuan Gobind Singh Deo]

Angka yang diberi (15)
  • 1.58 juta participants recorded on the digital learning portal
  • 11 learning modules on the digital learning portal
  • 100,000 youth eligible for free premium AI software access
  • tiga bulan duration of free premium AI software access
  • 11,979 youth who completed the six set modules
  • 9,182 youth who redeemed the software access
  • 100,000 Coursera licences provided in stages
  • 16,000 courses and professional certificates accessible through Coursera licences
  • 12,336 participations in the Coursera initiative
  • 13 cohorts of the Executive Digital Leadership Programme
  • 594 participants in the Executive Digital Leadership Programme
  • 490 public sector participants in the Executive Digital Leadership Programme
  • 104 private sector participants in the Executive Digital Leadership Programme
  • 40 Secretaries General and heads of public service who attended the leadership programme
  • 448,000 civil servants given access to Gemini Workspace
