  • 0.7 projected total fertility rate by 2050
  • 2025–2045 period covered by the National Ageing Action Plan
  • 2020–2060 period covered by the population projections report

Q (AI)

How effective are the measures taken by the ministry to control and curb the spread of invasive fish species harming local freshwater ecosystems?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Dr. Hj. Alias bin Razak [Kuala Nerus]

A (AI)

The minister cited freshwater landings, 31 hunting programmes with NGOs since 2022 that removed 8.8 metric tonnes of foreign fish, 101 inventory sessions during the Twelfth Malaysia Plan, public awareness and reporting channels such as MyFiRSt, 53 million native fish released, and proposed amendments to fisheries rules with fines up to RM20,000.

  • 9,434 metrik freshwater fisheries landings in 2023
  • 2.3 peratus increase in landings from 2023 to 2024
  • 31 siri hunting programmes carried out since 2022
  • 2022 year from which the hunting programmes were run
  • 8.8 tan metrik foreign fish removed through the hunting programmes
  • 101 siri freshwater fisheries inventory sessions during the Twelfth Malaysia Plan
  • 53 juta ekor native fish of various species released into public waters under RMK12
  • RM20,000 maximum fine proposed for releasing foreign fish
