Q (AI)

How far can B15 biodiesel reduce dependence on petroleum diesel, and will it affect engine performance, fuel use, maintenance costs and vehicle life?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Seri Utama Haji Aminuddin bin Harun [Port Dickson]

A (AI)

B15 from 1 June 2026 is meant to reduce petroleum diesel dependence; tests by MPOB and JAMA show blends up to B20 suit diesel vehicles, and the ministry says safety and performance concerns should no longer arise.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Perladangan dan Komoditi [Datuk Seri Dr. Noraini binti Ahmad]

Angka yang diberi (6)
  • 1 Jun 2026 Date the increase from B10 to B15 starts
  • Disember 2016 Date JAMA supported biodiesel up to B20
  • 2019 Year the ministry directed imported diesel vehicles to be B20-friendly
  • Januari 2020 Start date of the B20-friendly requirement for imported diesel vehicles
  • MS 123-5:2020 Malaysian standard that B7 to B20 blends comply with
  • 2020 Year B20 has been in use in Langkawi, Labuan and Sarawak except Bintulu

Q (AI)

How much diesel is expected to be saved by B15 in 2026 and what is the saving in value? Does the ministry have official confirmation from manufacturers such as Toyota that B15 will not affect performance, maintenance or warranty?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Seri Utama Haji Aminuddin bin Harun [Port Dickson]

A (AI)

The answer does not address the value of savings or manufacturer confirmation.

  • 100 peratus Biodiesel concentration FGV used in its Mazda CX-8
  • 67,000 kilometer Distance FGV has driven the Mazda CX-8 on biodiesel
