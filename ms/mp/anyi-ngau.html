Q (AI)

What steps will the ministry take to ensure subsidised goods are supplied in adequate quantity and quality, transparently and with integrity, in interior Sarawak such as Baram?

Ditanya oleh Dato Anyi Ngau [Baram]

A (AI)

RM250 million is allocated for 2026 to the Program Pengedaran Barang Perlu, which covers seven goods categories. Steps include SOPs for transporters, a monitoring committee, and periodic physical inspections by price monitoring and enforcement officers. Up to 30 September 2026, KPDN had monitored 67 of 139 points of sale in Baram and received two complaints, and points of sale found above controlled prices had their services terminated immediately.

What sections of the route lie in the Baram constituency?

Ditanya oleh Dato Anyi Ngau [Baram]

A (AI)
