Q (AI)

What enforcement action has been taken against traditional pig farms in Tanjung Sepat, and what measures are being taken to restore the polluted rivers and beaches?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Dr. Ahmad Yunus bin Hairi [Kuala Langat]

A (AI)

Enforcement falls under the Selangor state pig farming enactment and the state veterinary department, EIA reports apply only to new farms of 2,000 pigs or more, and NRES through JAS investigates complaints; long-term measures include tighter enforcement, integrated operations, targeted enforcement and expanded river water-quality monitoring.

  • 1974 Year of the Environmental Quality Act
