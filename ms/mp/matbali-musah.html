Q (AI)

How will the government improve governance in procurement and project distribution?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Matbali bin Musah [Sipitang]

A (AI)

Tenders are the preferred method. Bumiputera projects remain bumiputera but must go through tender, at least limited tender. Agent commissions for foreign workers will be cancelled through whitening and recalibration, a government procurement bill and an agency reform task force will be introduced, and 475 Chief Digital Officers will be appointed.

Dijawab oleh Perdana Menteri dan Menteri Kewangan [Dato' Seri Anwar bin Ibrahim]

Angka yang diberi (3)
  • RM20,000 hingga RM25,000 Amount an employer is said to pay for a domestic worker through agents
  • RM3,000 Maximum penalty for overstaying in Malaysia, as stated
  • 475 Number of Chief Digital Officers to be appointed
