Q (AI)

What is the status of the rural water and electricity projects for each listed village, and how will telecoms be coordinated?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohd Kurniawan Naim bin Moktar [Kinabatangan]

A (AI)

Three rural water projects in Kinabatangan were approved, with the Kuamut and Keramuak project (RM65 juta, 632 houses, eight villages) to be tendered next year over 24 months. Four of the 13 villages without electricity got supply from solar hybrid or diesel stations, and seven were removed from the final grid list. Telecoms fall under the Ministry of Communications, and the list was sent under JENDELA 2.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Kemajuan Desa dan Wilayah [Datuk Hajah Rubiah binti Haji Wang]

Q (AI)

What is the latest progress of SME Corp.'s PMKS status certification programme, and how does it help entrepreneurs access ministry aid and incentives?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohd Kurniawan Naim bin Moktar [Kinabatangan]

A (AI)

SME Corp. is the only agency mandated to certify PMKS status, which is needed for exemption from the minimum wage. Since 2020, 61,235 PMKS have been certified, and the application fee is being cut from RM100 to RM50 from tomorrow. Members are referred to the MyAssists and Hebatkan Perniagaan Malaysia portals for funding details.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Mohamad bin Alamin, Timbalan Menteri Pembangunan Usahawan dan Koperasi

Angka yang diberi (10)
  • 2020 year the PMKS certification was introduced
  • 61,235 number of PMKS that have obtained certified status since 2020
  • 17,000 PMKS certified in Selangor
  • 8,000 PMKS certified in Wilayah Persekutuan
  • 6,000 PMKS certified in Johor
  • 4,000 PMKS certified in Sabah (more than 4,000)
  • 61,000 total PMKS certified nationwide, as summarised
  • RM100 original PMKS certification application fee
  • RM50 reduced PMKS certification application fee
  • 50 peratus discount on the application fee

What is the plan to address the shortage of doctors in Sabah, and how many contract doctors are still waiting for permanent posts?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohd Kurniawan Naim bin Moktar [Kinabatangan]

Q (AI)

Will the government introduce a price adjustment mechanism to protect local contractors from sudden rises in construction material prices?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Mohd Kurniawan Naim bin Moktar [Kinabatangan]

A (AI)

The government has introduced short-, medium- and long-term interventions for contractors, including soft loans, interim payments twice a month, EOT extensions, contract flexibility, and a price-change clause (VOP) under consideration once diesel prices stabilise. As of 27 April 2026, 233 proposed projects worth 769.48 juta from 19 ministries could be deferred under RP1 2026, while nine ministries do not plan to defer any project.

Dijawab oleh Dato’ Indera Mohd Shahar bin Abdullah, Timbalan Menteri Ekonomi

  • 20 peratus cost increase threshold for contract flexibility (above 20 per cent for road maintenance works)
  • RM5 bilion additional financing guarantee under SJPP
  • 80 peratus government guarantee on financing for affected contractors
  • 10 tahun extended duration of the guarantee
  • dua kali sebulan frequency of interim payments
  • 27 April 2026 date of the Ministry of Economy's project count
  • 233 number of proposed projects that could be deferred
  • 769.48 juta ceiling value of the proposed deferred projects

  • sembilan number of ministries not planning to defer any project
