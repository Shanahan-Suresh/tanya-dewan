Q (AI)

Has the ministry considered raising the age limit of 60 for submitting Notis Keilatan claims to 65?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Seri Saravanan a/l Murugan [Tapah]

A (AI)

PERKESO sets no age limit for employment injury claims; the Pencen Ilat and Pencen Penakat age limit under Act 4 remains 60, changed from 55 in 2013; workers aged 60 and above in the formal sector are covered under the Skim Bencana Kerja and self-employed informal workers under Act 789.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Sumber Manusia [Dato’ Sri Abdul Rahman bin Haji Mohamad]

Angka yang diberi (3)
  • 60 tahun Age limit for Pencen Ilat and Pencen Penakat under Act 4, unchanged
  • 55 tahun Previous age limit for eligibility, changed in 2013
  • 2013 Year the age limit was changed from 55 to 60
