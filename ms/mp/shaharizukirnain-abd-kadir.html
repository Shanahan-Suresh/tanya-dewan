Dijawab oleh Puan Teo Nie Ching, Timbalan Menteri Komunikasi

  • RM1 juta maximum fine for non-compliance with a commission directive
  • 10 tahun maximum jail term for non-compliance
  • RM500,000 maximum financial penalty under section 105(5)
