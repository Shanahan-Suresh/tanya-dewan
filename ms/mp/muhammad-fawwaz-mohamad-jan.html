Q (AI)

What progress has the government made in ending extreme poverty, and what obstacles has the plan faced?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Haji Muhammad Fawwaz bin Mohamad Jan [Permatang Pauh]

A (AI)

Extreme poverty fell from 0.2 per cent in 2022 to 0.09 per cent in 2024 (HIES 2024). The answer cites RAHMAH cash and basic aid, Payung RAHMAH, welfare aid via the Department of Social Welfare, the Sejahtera MADANI programme, minimum wage revisions, and skills training. Poverty is dynamic, with households falling into poverty from factors such as loss of breadwinner, job loss or natural disasters.
