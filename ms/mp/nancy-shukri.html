Q (AI)

What is the difference between social work and volunteer work?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Sri Hajah Nancy binti Shukri

A (AI)

Volunteer work is sincere help given by individuals or organisations out of concern for others; care work focuses on physical personal care and daily needs; social work involves activities to respond to and improve the wellbeing of individuals, groups and communities, using defined methods and processes. The Bill does not cover or prevent volunteer work and care work.

Dijawab oleh Dato’ Sri Hajah Nancy binti Shukri, Menteri Pembangunan Wanita, Keluarga dan Masyarakat

Will JKM review the daycare SOP to allow more children than the existing ratio to offset rising costs?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Sri Hajah Nancy binti Shukri

A (AI)

Two factors apply: caregiver ratios under the 2012 regulations and minimum floor space per child. For example, non-OKU children aged three and above have a ratio of 10 children to one caregiver, and OKU children in the same age group have 10 children to three caregivers. Minimum floor space is 2.5 square metres for home daycares and 3.5 square metres for workplace, community or institutional daycares. JKM will examine the suggestion further.
