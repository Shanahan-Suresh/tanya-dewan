Ditanya oleh Tuan Ganabatirau a/l Veraman [Klang]

Q (AI)

Will the government conduct an independent investigation and national audit, identify those responsible, and disclose the findings?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Ganabatirau a/l Veraman [Klang]

A (AI)

Investigations and independent reviews have been carried out, including a briefing from SPRM. SPRM found that the gazettement of a retention pond had not been carried out during a land transfer, and the Federal Territories Land Committee cancelled a 1994 approval, leaving an 18-year delay. Two task forces were set up, 543 green areas were gazetted this year, and the number of flash flood hotspots fell from 30 to 14. On-site detention ponds were also built, with more under construction.

Dijawab oleh Puan Hannah Yeoh Tseow Suan, Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Wilayah Persekutuan)

What is the government's position on the perceived imbalance under section 103, and will law reform be considered?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Ganabatirau a/l Veraman [Klang]

Section 103 has applied since 1968 and is not punitive; it ensures orderly collection, and taxpayers' right to appeal after payment is preserved. Appeal cases are very few relative to total taxpayers, and the government will keep improving governance and internal review processes.
