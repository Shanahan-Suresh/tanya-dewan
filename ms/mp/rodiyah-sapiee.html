Q (AI)

What efforts, especially by the government, are being made to provide incentives to boost the hydrogen economy, given that hydrogen production needs very high electricity?

Ditanya oleh Puan Rodiyah binti Sapiee [Batang Sadong]

A (AI)

Hydrogen production need not require high electricity depending on the technology used. A HyPEReactor rural electrification project is planned, with RM2 juta allocated for 2026 and the location still being determined. MDV offers financing for green tech through a RM2 bilion SUKUK programme and incentive schemes, and the government has extended GITA and GITE to hydrogen technology, with ITA of 100 per cent over 10 years.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Sains, Teknologi dan Inovasi [Tuan Chang Lih Kang]

  • 2026 Year for which the HyPEReactor electrification initiative is allocated funds
  • RM2 juta Allocation for the HyPEReactor rural electrification initiative
  • 2022 Year MDV launched its SUKUK programme
  • RM2 bilion Value of the MDV SUKUK programme
  • tiga Number of government-supported programmes providing incentives with the Green Tech Financing Scheme
  • 148 Number of companies MDV has approved financing for green tech projects
  • RM2.26 bilion Total financing approved by MDV for green tech projects
