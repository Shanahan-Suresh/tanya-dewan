What post-disaster aid and modern drainage technology incentives are provided to paddy farmers and smallholders in Kota Marudu prone to floods?

Q (AI)

What is the current status of the Pan Borneo highway packages and the Kota Marudu dual carriageway, and what mitigation steps are being taken?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Wetrom bin Bahanda [Kota Marudu]

A (AI)

The Sabah section has 35 packages over 706 km; four are complete, 31 remain, with completion expected by 2030. Phase 1A is 89.01% physically complete and Phase 1B 23.96%. Nine more Phase 1A packages are expected by end-2026/early 2027. Mitigation includes task forces, agency meetings, site visits and punitive action against problem contractors. The Kota Marudu dual carriageway is a state road outside the Pan Borneo project.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Kerja Raya [Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan]

Angka yang diberi (10)
  • 706 total length in kilometres of the Sabah Pan Borneo alignment
  • 35 total number of work packages
  • 16 number of work packages in Phase 1A
  • 19 number of work packages in Phase 1B
  • empat number of packages already fully completed
  • 31 number of remaining packages
  • 2030 year by which all packages are expected to be complete
  • 89.01 peratus physical progress of Phase 1A
  • 23.96 peratus physical progress of Phase 1B
  • sembilan number of further Phase 1A packages expected to finish by end-2026/early 2027

Q (AI)

Are the delays caused by late payments to contractors, or by contractors lacking expertise and machinery, and what action will the ministry take?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Wetrom bin Bahanda [Kota Marudu]

A (AI)

Payments are not late. Phase 1A physical progress is 89.54% against a schedule of 96.23%, while payment is 88.78%. Phase 1B is at 25.53% against a schedule of 24.76%, and payment of 22.11% exceeds the scheduled 20.73%. Some contractors are problematic, which the minister does not deny; monthly meetings are now held, chaired by the state secretary and the ministry's secretary-general.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan, Timbalan Menteri Kerja Raya

  • 15 number of work packages stated for Phase 1A
  • 96.23 peratus scheduled physical progress for Phase 1A
  • 89.54 peratus actual physical progress for Phase 1A
  • 88.78 peratus financial payment progress for Phase 1A
  • tujuh peratus gap between scheduled and actual physical progress in Phase 1A
  • 24.76 peratus scheduled physical progress for Phase 1B
  • 25.53 peratus actual physical progress for Phase 1B
  • 20.73 peratus scheduled payment progress for Phase 1B
  • 22.11 peratus actual payment progress for Phase 1B

Q (AI)

What are the short- and long-term plans to raise cash crop yields in Kota Marudu, given unrepaired irrigation?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Wetrom bin Bahanda [Kota Marudu]

A (AI)

Sweet corn was planted on 19 acres from 2021 to 2023 under the Sabah Agriculture Department. Short-term expansion targets two hectares of corn and six hectares of vegetables in 2024. Long-term, 15.3 hectares are being guided towards myGAP certification. The Sabah irrigation department will implement the Marudu Irrigation Scheme Project costing RM5.86 million.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan [Datuk Seri Haji Mohamad bin Sabu]

Angka yang diberi (6)
  • 19 ekar area of sweet corn planted in Kota Marudu
  • 2021 sehingga 2023 period in which the sweet corn planting took place
  • dua hektar 2024 target for additional corn planting
  • enam hektar 2024 target for additional vegetable planting
  • 15.3 hektar area of cash crops being guided towards myGAP certification
  • RM5.86 juta cost of the Marudu Irrigation Scheme Project
