Q (AI)

What is the rationale and implication of transferring slope management responsibility to state governments, including the amount of funds transferred and the scope of responsibility?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

There is no comprehensive transfer of slope responsibility; responsibility depends on ownership, gazettement and asset jurisdiction. JKR remains responsible for federal road slopes. MARRIS grants under the Federal Constitution are not funds transferred from any new slope responsibility handover.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

Angka yang diberi (1)
  • Perkara 109(1)(b) Article of the Federal Constitution under which the MARRIS road maintenance grant is provided

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Has each state's financial capacity been assessed, taking into account development disparities and the high repair costs and uneven number of slopes between states?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

Slope numbers, risk levels, geology, rainfall intensity, development levels and mitigation costs differ between states. Failure of one critical slope may cost far more than routine maintenance. Road grants take into account eligible road length and average maintenance costs, not wealth alone. Slope management cannot rely on a financial formula alone, so JKR uses the PBRC risk-based approach.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Who bears full responsibility for slope disasters in prevention, emergency response, repair and cost, and does the law need amending?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

Responsibility is not vested in a single agency but follows each body's function and jurisdiction. NADMA Directive No. 1, effective from the stated date, coordinates disaster management. The Street, Drainage and Building Act 1974 provides for periodic slope inspection by local authorities. Law amendment is not necessarily needed for administrative coordination, but substantive changes to powers or duties would require proper legal review.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

Angka yang diberi (4)
  • 1 Ogos 2024 Date NADMA Directive No. 1 took effect
  • Arahan NADMA No. 1 NADMA directive on disaster management policy and mechanisms
  • Seksyen 85A Section of Act 133 allowing local authorities to require periodic slope inspection
  • Akta 133 Act number of the Street, Drainage and Building Act 1974

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Is there a guarantee that slope inspection, maintenance, repair, recovery and response time standards are uniform nationwide regardless of state finances or bureaucracy?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

Slope safety should be based on technical standards and risk levels, not on location or state finances alone. JKR has issued technical documents such as slope design and maintenance guidelines and SOPs. The slope hazard mapping guidelines launched in the stated year serve as a reference for federal, state and local agencies, subject to their acceptance. Uniform implementation will be strengthened through training, technical audits, data sharing, competency development and inter-agency coordination.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

Angka yang diberi (1)
  • 2024 Year the Slope Hazard Mapping and Assessment Guidelines were launched

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Do state governments have sufficient expertise, such as geotechnical engineers, civil engineers, geologists and disaster risk specialists, to carry out slope assessment and management if responsibility is transferred?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

The ministry acknowledges that technical expertise is not uniform across states and agencies. Slope management is collaborative, with state and district JKR playing key roles in inspection and monitoring, supported by CKC JKR and other agencies. Current expertise in geotechnical and civil engineering includes five officers and one officer on loan from JMG. The national plan uses a whole-of-government approach through ICSM, which now covers 39 agencies and five working groups.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

Angka yang diberi (4)
  • lima orang pegawai Number of geotechnical and civil engineering officers currently available for hazard assessment and mapping
  • satu pegawai pinjaman Number of officers on loan from JMG
  • 39 Number of agencies in the Inter-Governmental Agency Committee for Slope Management
  • lima kumpulan kerja Number of specialised working groups under ICSM

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Has the state's capacity to own and maintain technology such as LiDAR, GNSS, drones, IoT sensors, remote sensing and satellites been studied, including cost and specialist expertise?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

JKR already uses technologies such as LiDAR, GIS, GNSS, robotic total stations, inclinometers, tilt sensors, piezometers, soil-moisture sensors and IoT telemetry to monitor high-risk slopes. Data from some sites is sent in real time to control centres for early action. National sharing of assets, technology and expertise can optimise government investment and avoid duplicate facilities among states.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Is the government ready to establish a national slope expertise centre that pools geotechnical, geology, GIS, remote sensing, data and AI expertise and high-tech equipment for deployment to any state?

Ditanya oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

A (AI)

The ministry notes the proposal for a national slope expertise centre. Currently, national slope coordination and technical expertise are largely carried out by CKC JKR with ICSM and technical agencies. JPA previously proposed using the strengthened CKC JKR to build national slope expertise and coordination. The proposed centre could be considered by strengthening CKC JKR's existing ecosystem, without duplicating functions of existing technical agencies.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Kerja Raya [Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi]

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Will the government create a national database of high-risk slopes recording location, risk level, geology, landslide history, maintenance records and monitoring data?

Ditanya oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi

A (AI)

JKR's ISMAS, upgraded in 2025, manages and records slope inventory data on federal and state roads in Peninsular Malaysia, with related systems used in Sabah and Sarawak, and slopes repaired will be reassessed to update it.

Dijawab oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi, Menteri Kerja Raya

Angka yang diberi (1)
  • 2025 Year the ISMAS system was completed upgrading

8 Okt 2026

Q (AI)

Has the government developed a real-time early warning system for slopes that combines all the data needed to detect changes in slope stability before landslides?

Ditanya oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi

A (AI)

JKR has developed SAATR and real-time monitoring at high-risk slopes combining rainfall data and instruments, with 73 rain gauge stations nationwide and data integration with JPS and METMalaysia, but such a system cannot predict every landslide precisely in time and place.

Dijawab oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi, Menteri Kerja Raya

Angka yang diberi (3)
  • 73 Number of rain gauge stations in JKR's monitoring network across Malaysia
  • 2025-2030 Period covered by the Pelan Tindakan of the National Slope Master Plan (PICN)
  • Mei 2025 Month the PICN Action Plan was launched

Does the Patents Act provide for dealing with patented medicines and products sold at high prices? (as reported in the Minister's reply to Tebrau)

Ditanya oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi

A (AI)

The Patents Bill 2021 allows any person at any time after a patent is granted to apply for a compulsory licence. Making and producing the patented product in Malaysia without the owner's permission is not an infringement where the owner sells it at a high price in Malaysia.

Dijawab oleh Dato Sri Alexander Nanta Linggi, Menteri Perdagangan Dalam Negeri dan Hal Ehwal Pengguna
