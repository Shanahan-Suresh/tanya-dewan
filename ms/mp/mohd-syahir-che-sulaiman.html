Q (AI)

What strategy will the Government use to raise the compensation of employees share of GDP to 40 per cent by 2030, and how will wages keep pace with productivity?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Haji Mohd Syahir bin Che Sulaiman [Bachok]

A (AI)

RMK13 targets 40 per cent by 2030 and Ekonomi MADANI targets 45 per cent by 2033. The Government will move to high-value industries, coordinate wages through MPGN, review minimum wages, expand the progressive wage policy and continue PLWS.

Q (AI)

What is the latest assessment of the Dasar Gaji Progresif and its impact on SMEs, and are extra fiscal incentives planned?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Haji Mohd Syahir bin Che Sulaiman [Bachok]

A (AI)

DGP aims to raise worker incomes in line with productivity. RM200 million is allocated for full implementation in 2025, with incentives up to RM300 a month until 2027.
