Q (AI)

Will the government simplify procedures or create a fast track for urgent late registration cases, and how will agencies coordinate so applicants need not resubmit documents?

Ditanya oleh Dr. Haji Abd Ghani bin Ahmad [Jerlun]

A (AI)

Late registrations have several causes, including parental awareness, family problems, finances, and incomplete supporting documents. The registration period is 60 days in the Peninsula and 42 days in Sabah and Sarawak. DNA results are supporting information, not automatic proof of citizenship. A new SOP has been set and processing powers devolved to all offices. The MP was asked to submit the case in detail.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Seri Dr. Shamsul Anuar bin Haji Nasarah, Timbalan Menteri Dalam Negeri

Angka yang diberi (2)
  • 60 hari registration period for births in the Peninsula
  • 42 hari registration period for births in Sabah and Sarawak
