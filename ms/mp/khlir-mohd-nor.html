Q (AI)

How many trainees have been trained so far, and when can the training camps be used?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Khlir bin Mohd Nor [Ketereh]

A (AI)

About 3,404 trainees have been trained so far, with about 4,200 expected by year-end at three camps. Nine more camps are in progress and up to 9,200 trainees are expected next year. Q (AI)

Why has MAT not acted on pension adjustment for veterans who retired before 2013, and should MAT be given power to make the amendment?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Khlir bin Mohd Nor [Ketereh]

A (AI)

MAT was set up under Article 137 and pension adjustment falls under the 1982 Regulations. As at 17 October 2025, 33,631 veterans and dependants had been considered for BSH, including 2,317 pensioners, and eligibility is based on the poverty line.

Dijawab oleh Tuan Haji Adly bin Zahari, Timbalan Menteri Pertahanan

  • 2,317 veteran pensioners among those considered for BSH
  • 17 Oktober 2025 date up to which the BSH figures were counted
