A (AI)

Under TRF 2.0, Malaysia attracted 10.0 juta foreign tourists and RM28.2 bilion in tourism receipts in 2022, against targets of 9.2 juta and RM26.2 bilion. This year RM75.64 juta was allocated for tourism infrastructure upgrading and renovation, up RM21.34 juta from RM54.3 juta last year, plus a special budget of RM30 juta whose details have not yet been given.
