Q (AI)

How effective is coordination between KKM, KPM, KBS and KPKT under the national haze action plan in protecting at-risk groups, and will SOPs such as work from home, online PdP and alternative arrangements for SPM and STPM be introduced?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Haji Manndzri bin Haji Nasib [Tenggara]

A (AI)

JAS monitors air quality and issues IPU readings, while the health and education ministries and others take protective measures within their remits. NADMA will be activated when the IPU exceeds 150 for more than 24 hours, and the plan has been revised several times. Work from home, PdP and examination SOPs fall under the relevant ministries' jurisdiction, notably KPM and KPT.

Dijawab oleh Dato’ Sri Arthur Joseph Kurup, Menteri Sumber Asli dan Kelestarian Alam

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • 150 IPU level above which NADMA is activated if sustained
  • 24 jam duration the IPU must stay above the threshold before NADMA is activated
  • enam kali number of times the haze action plan has been revised
  • 2002 year from which the plan has been revised
  • 2025 year of the most recent revision of the plan

19 Okt 2026Pertanyaan-Pertanyaan bagi Jawab Lisan

Q (AI)

What is the current safety status and periodic monitoring of Linggiu Dam, and how effective is the dam safety flying squad in auditing its structural integrity?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Haji Manndzri bin Haji Nasib [Tenggara]

A (AI)

The dam is overseen by Singapore's PUB under the 1962 agreement and its 1990 supplementary agreement, with yearly and five-yearly inspections. The flying squad operated from 2018 to 2022 and inspected 41 dams. PEN, set up in 2025, monitors 107 dams, but Linggiu is outside its scope, so requests go through the Johor water regulatory body and PUB Singapore.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Amar Haji Fadillah bin Haji Yusof, Timbalan Perdana Menteri dan Menteri Peralihan Tenaga dan Transformasi Air

Angka yang diberi (7)
  • 1962 Year of the Johor River Water Agreement
  • 1990 Year of the supplementary agreement
  • lima tahun Full inspection interval of the dam by PUB
  • 2018 hingga 2022 Period the flying squad operated
  • 41 empangan Number of dams inspected by the flying squad
  • 2025 Year the National Dam Centre (PEN) was established
  • 107 empangan Number of dams monitored by PEN
