Q (AI)

What is the status of the Advanced Packaging initiative under MSE, and what role does MAPC play in strengthening Malaysia's semiconductor ecosystem?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Wira Dr. Ku Abd Rahman bin Ku Ismail [Kubang Pasu]

A (AI)

MAPC, a consortium of five local semiconductor companies, has run an integrated RDICE programme since March 2026 with five sub-projects to produce Malaysia's first HBM4 advanced packaging chip, with matching funding from the companies and MSE, aiming to reach seven percent of the global advanced packaging market.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Sains, Teknologi dan Inovasi [Datuk Chang Lih Kang]

Angka yang diberi (7)
  • tujuh peratus target share of the global advanced packaging market
  • Lima number of local semiconductor companies forming MAPC
  • Mac 2026 start of the integrated RDICE programme under MAPC
  • lima sub projek number of sub-projects in the integrated programme
  • RM185 juta total value of the project
  • RM93 juta matching funds from the participating companies
  • RM92 juta matching funds from MSE

Q (AI)

What is the ratio of STEM to non-STEM entrants to IPTA/IPTS, and how many engineers are targeted under DSTIN 2021-2030?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Wira Dr. Ku Abd Rahman bin Ku Ismail [Kubang Pasu]

A (AI)

From 2023 to March 2026, IPTA admitted 556,556 diploma and first-degree students, 58.6 per cent in STEM and 41.4 per cent in non-STEM. DSTIN targets 200 RSET per 10,000 workers by 2030, not a fixed number of engineers per discipline, which gives about 341,200 RSET workers needed by 2030.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Pendidikan Tinggi [Tuan Adam Adli Abd Halim]

Angka yang diberi (8)
  • 556,556 total diploma and first-degree entrants to IPTA, 2023 to March 2026
  • 326,419 entrants in STEM fields
  • 58.6 peratus share of entrants in STEM fields
  • 230,137 entrants in non-STEM fields
  • 41.4 peratus share of entrants in non-STEM fields
  • 200 RSET target per 10,000 workers by 2030
  • 17.06 juta projected national workforce
  • 341,200 RSET workers needed by 2030
