What is the status of fair compensation for Gerik plantation owners and breeders whose crops and livestock were destroyed by wildlife?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Fathul Huzir bin Ayob [Gerik]

  • empat, lima tahun Approximate time, four to five years, for the full implementation
