What are the case numbers, seizure values and arrests under Ops Tiris Bersepadu, especially at borders, and what causes repeated smuggling?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Sri Sh Mohmed Puzi bin Sh Ali [Pekan]

KPDN recorded 125,690 inspections, 1,542 cases, RM59.9 million in seizures and 514 arrests; border cases were 357 (RM10.77 million) and sea cases 275 (RM12.29 million). Q (AI)

What is Malaysia's position on the imbalance of veto power in the UN Security Council, and what strategy will it use to push for UN reform and a bigger Malaysian role?

Ditanya oleh Dato' Sri Sh Mohmed Puzi bin Sh Ali [Pekan]

A (AI)

Malaysia calls for comprehensive Security Council reform including the veto, which it considers unfair and says should be abolished at least for atrocity crimes. It cites UNGA Resolution 76/262 of 2022, a recent emergency UNGA session, its non-permanent seat terms, and its nomination for 2036 to 2037.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Luar Negeri [Dato Lukanisman bin Awang Sauni]

  • 10 hari bekerja working days within which the UN General Assembly must meet after a veto is used
  • 16 April 2026 date of an emergency UN General Assembly session after a veto
  • 7 April 2026 date China and Russia used the veto on a resolution on Iran and the Strait of Hormuz
  • empat kali number of times Malaysia has served as a non-permanent Security Council member
  • 2036 hingga 2037 term for which Malaysia has nominated itself as a non-permanent Security Council member
  • September 2026 month of the UN General Assembly general debate in New York
  • Ke-81 81st session of the UN General Assembly

Q (AI)

Is Malaysia ready to lead a firmer and more concrete push for UN Security Council reform, given that the veto is misused as a tool of oppression?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Sri Sh Mohmed Puzi bin Sh Ali [Pekan]

A (AI)

Malaysia maintains its firm position that the veto is unfair and should be abolished. Its proposals include abolishing the veto, conditional use of the veto until abolition (for atrocity crimes), transparent explanation in negotiations, close cooperation with supporting countries, and calling on the international community to intensify UN reform.

Dijawab oleh Dato Lukanisman bin Awang Sauni, Timbalan Menteri Luar Negeri
