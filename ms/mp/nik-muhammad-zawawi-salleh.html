What is the basis for keeping national debt around 60 to 65 per cent of GDP amid geopolitical uncertainty and trade competition?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Dr. Nik Muhammad Zawawi bin Salleh [Pasir Puteh]

A (AI)

Federal government debt was 63.9 per cent of GDP at end-June 2025, within the 65 per cent statutory limit. The government aims for a 3.8 per cent deficit this year and, over the medium term, a deficit below 3 per cent and debt below 60 per cent of GDP.

Q (AI)

What mitigation steps has the ministry taken to keep agriculture from being affected by floods?

Ditanya oleh Datuk Dr. Nik Muhammad Zawawi bin Haji Salleh [Pasir Puteh]

A (AI)

The ministry has maintained irrigation and drainage systems, allocated funds, kept pumps and tidal gates working, and is developing a flood-tolerant rice variety and a GIS-based database with other agencies.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan [Datuk Seri Haji Mohamad bin Sabu]

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • RM55 juta Amount allocated in 2025 for maintaining agricultural irrigation and drainage systems nationwide
  • RM14.8 juta Part of the RM55 million channelled specifically to the East Coast state
  • 14 hari Number of days the MARDI submergence-gene rice variety can survive under water
  • 2028 Year the flood-tolerant rice variety is expected to be introduced
  • lima juta kampit Equivalent of rice yield lost in flooding in Pasir Mas, Tumpat and Kedah
