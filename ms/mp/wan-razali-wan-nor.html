Q (AI)

What help does the ministry give the batik industry in Kuantan, including financial aid to raise capacity, competitiveness and marketing?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Wan Razali bin Wan Nor [Kuantan]

A (AI)

Kraftangan supports batik entrepreneurs through skills, product, promotion and marketing programmes. The ministry has no funds itself, but RM10 million is available through the Ministry of Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives, via TEKUN Nasional, at 4% interest for loans up to RM100,000 repayable over 10 years.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Pelancongan, Seni dan Budaya [Dato Sri Tiong King Sing]

  • 10 tahun repayment period of up to 10 years

Q (AI)

Why did flooding still occur in Kuantan after the Sungai Belat flood mitigation project was completed?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Wan Razali bin Wan Nor [Kuantan]

A (AI)

Flooding was caused by very high rainfall, with 185 mm recorded at the Sungai Belat station at Sri Damai. Flood projects are designed to a set capacity, and extreme rainfall can exceed it.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Peralihan Tenaga dan Transformasi Air [Dato’ Sri Haji Abdul Rahman bin Haji Mohamad]

Angka yang diberi (2)
  • 185 mm Rainfall recorded at the Sungai Belat station at Sri Damai
  • 60mm Threshold above which rainfall is classed as very heavy
