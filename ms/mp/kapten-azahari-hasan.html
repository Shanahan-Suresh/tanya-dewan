Q (AI)

What is the current status of federal road upgrades in Padang Rengas, and what steps are being taken to reduce accidents, including street lighting?

Ditanya oleh Kapten Azahari bin Hasan [Padang Rengas]

A (AI)

Street lighting was installed at 75 selected Perak locations at a cost of RM7.85 juta in 2023 to 2025. Three Padang Rengas sites were completed with 29 solar LED lamps costing RM300 ribu, and three more sites with an estimated 22 units costing RM230 ribu are under work, with physical works to be completed this year.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Kerja Raya [Datuk Seri Dr. Haji Ahmad bin Haji Maslan]

  • 29 batang number of solar LED street lamps installed at the completed sites
  • RM300 ribu cost of the completed Padang Rengas sites

Q (AI)

What is Malaysia's progress on international climate and environmental commitments, and its strategy for balancing economic development with global responsibility?

Ditanya oleh Kapten Azahari bin Hasan [Padang Rengas]

A (AI)

Malaysia is committed to climate action and has joined the Paris Agreement. Its third NDC was submitted to UNFCCC in October 2025, a 2050 net zero aspiration is set, DPIN 2.0 was launched in 2024, and green investment and the Article 6 mechanism are cited as drivers of growth. A Climate Change Bill and a climate change authority bill will be drafted.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Sumber Asli dan Kelestarian Alam [Tuan Syed Ibrahim bin Syed Noh]

Angka yang diberi (13)
  • lima tahun sekali NDC emissions reduction targets are set every five years

  • Bagi tahun 2025 year referred to for Malaysia's recent achievements
  • salah satu daripada lima negara ASEAN terawal Malaysia was among the first five ASEAN countries to submit its third NDC
  • Oktober tahun lepas month in which the third NDC was submitted to UNFCCC
  • 15 hingga 30 tan karbon dioksida setara absolute GHG emissions reduction target under the new NDC
  • menjelang tahun 2035 target year for the new NDC reduction
  • 30 Oktober 2025 date Host Party Participation form was submitted to UNFCCC
  • menjelang tahun 2050 target year for net zero GHG emissions aspiration
  • 2 darjah Celsius global average temperature rise limit that the commitment aims to keep below
  • pada tahun 2024 year DPIN 2.0 was launched
  • RM2.3 hingga RM2.6 trilion expected green investment
  • tahun 2023 hingga tahun 2050 period covered by the green investment expectation

Q (AI)

Does the two per cent tax on LLP profits above RM100,000 cause double taxation and why is it imposed?

Ditanya oleh Kapten Azahari bin Hasan [Padang Rengas]

A (AI)

The tax equalises treatment between LLP partners and individual shareholders after the dividend tax; double taxation does not arise because the LLP pays company-rate tax and partners pay the two per cent; the two per cent is a minimum rate with the threshold not expected to burden partners.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Kewangan [Puan Lim Hui Ying]

  • 2026 year of assessment from which the tax applies
  • 2025 year of assessment from which the two per cent dividend tax applied
  • 118 number of LLPs
  • 724 number of partners
