Q (AI)

Is RM100 sufficient given rising costs, and will physical bookshops be allowed to participate, especially in rural areas?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Kalam bin Salan [Sabak Bernam]

A (AI)

RM100 is within the government's existing financial capacity, and RM230 million has been allocated this year for KPM institutions, excluding higher education institutions. Physical redemption is not being pursued because online purchase ensures all vouchers are redeemed and bought with no leakage; several constituencies, including Beaufort, Labuan and Miri, reached 100 per cent redemption.

Dijawab oleh Tuan Wong Kah Woh, Timbalan Menteri Pendidikan

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • RM100 value of voucher
  • RM230 juta allocation this year for institutions under KPM
  • 100 peratus redemption reached in several constituencies
  • kosong peratus leakage rate over three years
  • 100 peratus transparency rate over three years

Will the government set a fish catch size so that only mature fish may be caught?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Kalam bin Salan [Sabak Bernam]

A (AI)

No direct fish size limit is used; instead, trawl mesh size rules are enforced, along with education, and DOF has compounded cases and deployed artificial reefs.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan [Dato’ Sri Arthur Joseph Kurup]

Angka yang diberi (6)
  • 38 milimeter Minimum mesh size at the codend of mandatory trawl nets, enforced since 1 November 2013
  • 1.5 inci The same minimum trawl mesh size expressed in inches
  • 1 November 2013 Date the trawl mesh size rule was enforced
  • 55 kes Fishing catch cases compounded for using trawl nets with mesh under 38 mm, 2019 to September 2025
  • 834 unit Artificial reef (tukun) units deployed nationwide
  • RM19.9 juta Value of the artificial reef units deployed, 2021 to 2025

Q (AI)

Asks the Minister of Transport to state the consideration given to using PAKEJ three-wheel vehicles and their use by farmers and smallholders to carry produce.

Ditanya oleh Tuan Kalam bin Salan [Sabak Bernam]

A (AI)

Three-wheel motorcycles are regulated under the Road Transport Act 1987 and the 1959 Rules; goods use requires registration as a goods vehicle, VTA approval and six-monthly inspection, and JPJ will help with the VTA process.

Dijawab oleh Menteri Pengangkutan [Tuan Loke Siew Fook]

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • 66(1) Section of the Road Transport Act 1987 allowing rules on vehicle use
  • 1987 Year of the Road Transport Act
  • 121A Rule in the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Rules
  • 1959 Year of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Rules
  • enam bulan Six-monthly periodic inspection at the motor vehicle inspection centre
