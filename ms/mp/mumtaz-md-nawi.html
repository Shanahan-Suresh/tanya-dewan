Q (AI)

What justifies the tariff increase affecting domestic and non-domestic users, how does the government assess people’s ability to cope with higher living costs, and how does it ensure AFA cost changes are not passed on excessively to consumers?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Hajah Mumtaz binti Md Nawi [Tumpat]

A (AI)

Higher bills are due to global fuel prices, restricted LNG shipments through the Strait of Hormuz, low hydro output, coal plant outages and high usage from hotter weather linked to El Niño. AFA was introduced in July 2025 and the Energy Commission checks monthly costs; AFA adjustments are automatic only up to a 10 per cent threshold, with any excess possibly covered by the Electricity Industry Fund. Over 7.4 million domestic users below 600 kWh a month are protected, and the government extended the exemption threshold to 800 kWh a month for September to December 2026.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Peralihan Tenaga dan Transformasi Air [Dato’ Sri Haji Abdul Rahman bin Haji Mohamad]

Angka yang diberi (10)
  • 7.4 juta number of domestic electricity users protected (over 7.4 million)
  • 85 peratus share of domestic electricity users in Peninsular Malaysia protected
  • 600 kilowatt jam sebulan original monthly consumption threshold for protection
  • 10 peratus threshold up to which AFA adjustments are applied automatically
  • 18 September 2026 date the government agreed to extend the exemption threshold
  • 800 kilowatt jam sebulan new monthly consumption threshold for exemption
  • September hingga Disember 2026 period the extended exemption applies
  • 90 peratus approximate share of domestic accounts in Peninsular Malaysia benefiting from the extension
  • lapan juta approximate number of domestic accounts benefiting from the extension
  • April 2027 month until which the El Niño weather is forecast to persist
