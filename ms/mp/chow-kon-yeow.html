Which countries are in labour talks, how many workers are expected, and over what timeframe will industry shortages be addressed?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Chow Kon Yeow [Batu Kawan]

A (AI)

Eight MOUs with source countries are in force, India and Sri Lanka MOUs have expired, a new India MOU was finalised, and 995,396 quotas were approved with 84.7 percent levies paid; employers have up to 18 months to bring workers in.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Sumber Manusia [Tuan Haji Mustapha @ Mohd Yunus bin Sakmud]

  • 10 number of source countries with which Malaysia has had labour MOUs
  • lapan number of MOUs still in force (eight)
  • 13 Mac 2023 date the MOU with the Government of India was finalised
  • 995,396 foreign worker quotas approved as at 14 March 2023
  • 84.7 peratus share of approved quotas for which employers have paid levies
  • 18 bulan maximum period employers are given to bring in workers after paying levy
