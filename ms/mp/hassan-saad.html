Q (AI)

Will the government raise the takmir teacher allowance to RM1,200 a month?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Hassan bin Saad [Baling]

A (AI)

The allowance is financial assistance, not a fixed salary. Takmir teachers are paid per class at RM100, the claim ceiling was raised to RM900, and the quota was raised from 4,000 to 5,000. KAFA teachers receive RM1,100 a month and the imam allowance was RM850. Increases depend on the government's financial capacity.

Dijawab oleh Dr. Zulkifli bin Hasan, Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Hal Ehwal Agama)

  • 4,000 previous quota of takmir teachers
  • 5,000 raised quota of takmir teachers
  • RM1,100 monthly allowance for KAFA teachers
  • RM850 monthly imam allowance
  • 4,752 number of takmir teachers currently serving
  • 34,284 number of KAFA teachers currently serving

Q (AI)

Will a KWSP or SOCSO scheme be set up for takmir teachers, and will hours be raised so KAFA teachers earn at least RM1,200?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Hassan bin Saad [Baling]

A (AI)

There is currently no plan for a PERKESO social protection scheme for takmir teachers because they have other jobs. Raising 5,000 takmir teachers to RM1,200 would cost about RM72 million, a rise of over 33 per cent, so the government raised the quota from 4,000 to 5,000 instead.

Dijawab oleh Dr. Zulkifli bin Hasan, Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Hal Ehwal Agama)

Angka yang diberi (5)
  • 5,000 number of takmir teachers in the cost estimate
  • RM1,200 proposed monthly allowance in the cost estimate
  • RM72 juta estimated additional allowance cost
  • 33 peratus percentage increase in the allowance
  • 4,000 previous quota of takmir teachers
