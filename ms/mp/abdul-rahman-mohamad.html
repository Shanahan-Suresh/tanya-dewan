Q (AI)

What is the current status, capacity, budget and timeline of the LRA Batu Balai project, and what is being done about water supply in Kampung Baharu and Taman Inderapura?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Ir. Ts. Haji Khairil Nizam bin Khirudin [Jerantut]

A (AI)

PAIP is handling Kampung Baharu's supply from the Batu Balai plant, which runs above design capacity, and is planning pipe replacement; Taman Inderapura's low pressure is due to asbestos cement pipe bursts, with about 205 km of old pipe to replace at an estimated cost. Batu Embun Phase 5 was approved by SPAN and its tender is expected in March 2027; the Batu Balai upgrade under KKDW is in final design with tender expected in December 2026.

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Sri Haji Abdul Rahman bin Haji Mohamad
