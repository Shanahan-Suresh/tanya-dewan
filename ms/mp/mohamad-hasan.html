Q (AI)

What is Malaysia doing to help Timor-Leste become a full ASEAN member?

Ditanya oleh Dato’ Seri Utama Haji Mohamad bin Haji Hasan

A (AI)

Timor-Leste has sought full membership since 2011 and became an observer state in 2022. It must meet 88 instruments, most of them economic. Malaysia proposed that it need only meet 20 to 30 per cent for now, and Malaysia funded a Timor-Leste unit at the ASEAN Secretariat in Jakarta to help it meet the requirements.

Dijawab oleh Dato’ Seri Utama Haji Mohamad bin Haji Hasan, Menteri Luar Negeri

  • 2011 year Timor-Leste expressed its wish to join ASEAN as a full member
  • 2022 year ASEAN leaders agreed to make Timor-Leste an observer state
  • 88 total number of instruments Timor-Leste must meet
  • 69 number of the 88 instruments in the economic sector
  • 20 ke 30 peratus share of instruments Malaysia proposed Timor-Leste meet for now
  • USD200 ribu Malaysia's financial contribution to set up the Timor-Leste unit
