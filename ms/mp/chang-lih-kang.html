Q (AI)

Why has ratification taken so long, and is the ministry confident of meeting the schedule?

Ditanya oleh Tuan Tan Kar Hing [Gopeng]

A (AI)

Malaysia follows a dualist system so domestic law comes first; Act 834 was gazetted and fully enforced, a working committee was set up in 2023, and the Bahagian Penguasa Angkasa needs time to implement its recommendations.

Dijawab oleh Datuk Chang Lih Kang, Menteri Sains, Teknologi dan Inovasi

Angka yang diberi (4)
  • 25 Januari 2022 date Act 834 (Akta Lembaga Angkasa Malaysia 2022) was gazetted
  • 1 Januari 2025 date Act 834 was fully enforced
  • 2023 year the Jawatankuasa Kerja Triti Angkasa was set up
  • 19 number of ministries, government agencies and academia on the working committee
