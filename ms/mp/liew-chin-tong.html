Ditanya oleh Datuk Hajah Siti Aminah binti Aching [Beaufort]

A (AI)

As at 12 October 2026, 52,133 owners in Sabah were approved for the extra 100 litres. The government extended eligibility transfers from 16 September 2026, and eligible owners may pass eligibility to other Malaysians with a valid MyKad and BUDI95 eligibility, with up to 3 individuals for owners of more than one private diesel vehicle.

Dijawab oleh Timbalan Menteri Kewangan [Tuan Liew Chin Tong]

  • 12 Oktober 2026 date up to which the approval figures are given
  • RM2.10 price per litre of subsidised diesel at the pump
  • 300 liter basic monthly eligibility per individual
  • 100 liter extra monthly eligibility for eligible pickup and jeep owners
  • 400 liter total monthly eligibility with the extra 100 litres
  • 16 September 2026 date from which BUDI Diesel eligibility transfer was expanded
  • 19,008 number of BUDI Diesel eligibility transfer applications approved as at 12 October 2026
  • 5,555 number of approved transfer applications from Sabah
  • 3 maximum number of other individuals to whom eligibility may be transferred for owners of more than one private diesel vehicle
