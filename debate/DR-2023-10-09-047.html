Summary (AI)

What happened

Suhaizan bin Kaiat [Pulai] asked Datuk Seri Haji Mohamad bin Sabu for his view on a remark about FAMA distributing rice nationwide, and Sabu replied that he would not comment on remarks made outside Parliament. Dato' Dr. Zulkafperi bin Hanapi [Tanjong Karang] asked why BERNAS had not played a role in the local white rice shortage and why FAMA and LPP had been drawn into distributing rice, and Sabu answered that it was intervention, that BERNAS plays a role through importing, and that the government had set a purchase floor price of RM1,200 since 2014. Sabu said he would discuss the floor price urgently so that it could be reviewed. Mohd Misbahul Munir bin Masduki [Parit Buntar] said there was no local rice in Parit Buntar, mostly repackaged imported rice, and asked why FAMA-distributed rice was not sent to shops; Sabu explained that rising import prices led people to buy local rice in bulk, causing the shortage. Dato' Siti Zailah binti Mohd Yusoff [Rantau Panjang] asked about negotiations with India, Thailand and Vietnam, and Sabu said Cambodia, Vietnam and Thailand had given assurances at the ASEAN agriculture ministers' meeting in Kuala Lumpur. Ahmad Tarmizi bin Sulaiman [Sik] asked for clarification on government premises' purchases of local rice and questioned whether the 400 juta subsidy could solve the problem in the long term.
