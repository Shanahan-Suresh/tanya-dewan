Penerangan Daripada Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan di Bawah Peraturan Mesyuarat 14(1)(i) Isu Bekalan Beras Putih Tempatan

Summary (AI)

What happened

Datuk Seri Johari bin Abdul Ghani [Titiwangsa] said he supported the minister's intervention after the Deputy Speaker, Puan Alice Lau Kiong Yieng, had called time. Datuk Seri Dr. Ronald Kiandee [Beluran] agreed there was no rice shortage, but said the problem was a shortage of local white rice (BPT) supply rather than distribution, and that FAMA and LPP distribution did not address it. He said the mill buying price of RM1700 per tonne was above the RM1200 floor price set for paddy, and called for the ministry to examine possible manipulation, including the risk that seed paddy could be sold as local rice. Datuk Awang bin Hashim [Pendang] interjected twice, then said manufacturers did not pack local white rice but stockpiled it, and that rice lasted only six months. The Deputy Speaker, Dato' Ramli bin Dato' Mohd Nor, asked the Member for Pendang to wind up, and Datuk Seri Dr. Ronald Kiandee [Beluran] then closed by asking the ministry to examine all elements of manipulation and whether the SSL figure was 63 or 65 percent or lower.
