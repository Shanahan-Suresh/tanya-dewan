Summary (AI)

What happened

Tuan Karupaiya Mutusami (Padang Serai) asked SOCSO to liaise with JPN so that it could alert the families of deceased contributors, citing a case where a claim of RM34,300.12 was made after eight years, and asked about police assistance personnel who have no SOCSO or EPF. Dato’ Mahfuz bin Haji Omar replied that PERKESO's Skuad Prihatin checks incidents and contributions quickly, and asked MPs to urge employers to contribute. Dato’ Dr. Noraini Ahmad (Parit Sulong) raised school security guards whose wages had SOCSO deducted but who were found ineligible to claim after accidents, and asked for advice to employers. Dato’ Mahfuz said an employee whose payslip shows SOCSO deductions is eligible to claim, that action may be taken against employers, and advised filing an early report. He then said the government is committed to raising the minimum wage to RM1,500 as in the Pakatan Harapan manifesto within five years, and set RM1,050 a month or RM5.05 an hour effective 1 January 2019. Tuan Hassan bin Abdul Karim (Pasir Gudang) asked whether the government would consider the RM1,170 recommended by MPGN instead of RM1,050, and Dato’ Mahfuz said the Cabinet would decide. Puan Hajah Siti Zailah binti Mohd Yusoff (Rantau Panjang) said her question on dismissals had not been answered, and Dato’ Mahfuz said he would reply in writing because time was insufficient.

Buat pertama kalinya juga kerajaan berjaya menetapkan kadar gaji minimum yang seragam bagi seluruh negara termasuk Sabah dan Sarawak yang diseragamkan bersama dengan Semenanjung.
