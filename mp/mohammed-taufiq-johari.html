Q (AI)

What happens to the assets of a deregistered sports body, and why are bodies deregistered?

Asked by Menteri Belia dan Sukan [Dr. Mohammed Taufiq bin Johari]

A (AI)

Assets must be frozen and reported under the existing system; a new section 25A is proposed; bodies are deregistered for failing to comply with their constitution and good governance, not for inactivity; and a notice of at least a reasonable period, usually not less than 30 days, is given before further action.

Answered by Menteri Belia dan Sukan [Dr. Mohammed Taufiq bin Johari]

Figures given (3)
  • 25A proposed new section on the effects of deregistration of a sports body
  • 576 number of the Akta Pembangunan Sukan 1997
  • 30 hari minimum notice period given to a sports body to respond before further action
