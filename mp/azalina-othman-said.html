Q (AI)

How can it be ensured that the compensation award under fasal 42 is paid to the victim, and what is the position if the respondent cannot pay and why is the double-compensation penalty under fasal 44 imposed?

Asked by Datuk Che Mohamad Zulkifly bin Jusoh [Besut]

A (AI)

Fasal 43 gives the tribunal power to order compensation as a civil remedy, while fasal 44 enforces against deliberate non-compliance; double compensation is not automatic but a deterrent, and the tribunal can assess the respondent's actual circumstances before recommending further action under fasal 44. The minister said written answers would follow.
