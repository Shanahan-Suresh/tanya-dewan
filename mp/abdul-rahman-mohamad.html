What is the current status, capacity, budget and timeline of the LRA Batu Balai project, and what is being done about water supply in Kampung Baharu and Taman Inderapura?

Asked by Tuan Ir. Ts. Haji Khairil Nizam bin Khirudin [Jerantut]

A (AI)
