Asked by Ir. Ts. Haji Khairil Nizam bin Khirudin [Jerantut]

  • RM4 juta project cost approved by the government in Budget 2027
  • RM1 juta allocation ceiling for 2027
  • RM4 juta balance to be borne by FELCRA Berhad from internal sources
  • empat tahun expected implementation period
  • Mei 2027 planned start of implementation
