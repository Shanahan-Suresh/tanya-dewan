Q (AI)

What action has been taken on AI misuse and deepfakes, including requests to remove fake content?

Asked by Menteri Komunikasi [Datuk Ahmad Fahmi bin Mohamed Fadzil]

A (AI)

From 1 January 2022 to 15 January 2026, MCMC made requests to remove fake image and deepfake content, and most were removed. For false content, most requests were also actioned. Under amended section 233(1) of Act 588, fraud-related false content carries penalties.

Answered by Menteri Komunikasi [Datuk Ahmad Fahmi bin Mohamed Fadzil]

Figures given (9)
  • 5,409 requests submitted by MCMC to service providers to remove fake image and recent deepfake content, 1 January 2022 to 15 January 2026
  • 4,955 of those requests, content removed
  • 92 peratus percentage of those requests where content was removed
  • 59,967 requests submitted for false content
  • 51,912 requests where false content was removed
  • 87 peratus percentage of false-content requests where content was removed
  • RM500,000 maximum fine for fraud-related false content under amended section 233(1) of Act 588
  • dua tahun maximum prison term for that offence
  • RM5,000 additional daily fine for each day the offence continues after conviction
