What is the current status and preliminary findings of the Jalan Sungai Ujong traffic study, will it be fully implemented, and when will results be announced?

Asked by Tuan Cha Kee Chin [Rasah]

A (AI)

The traffic light junction closure ran from 30 September to 13 October 2026 and has ended.
