Q (AI)

How will the AI governance bill address copyright and intellectual property issues from AI systems trained on copyrighted data?

Asked by Tuan Suhaizan bin Kaiat [Pulai]

A (AI)

The bill is a framework law covering the whole AI lifecycle; it does not replace the Copyright Act 1987, requires managing legal and ethical risks including copyright infringement, and enforcement stays under existing law administered by MyIPO.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri Digital [Datuk Wilson Ugak anak Kumbong]

Figures given (1)
  • 1987 Year of the Copyright Act 1987, which the bill does not replace
