Q (AI)

Does the government plan to make health checks mandatory for licence renewal from age 70, and how would this affect road safety and elderly mobility?

Asked by Tuan Mohd Nazri bin Abu Hassan [Merbok]

A (AI)

Health checks are not currently required for private vehicle licence renewal at age 70 and above. Police data show a small share of accidents involve those aged 70 and above, and MIROS found no conclusive evidence that age-based mandatory checks significantly reduce accidents.
