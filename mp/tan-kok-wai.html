Q (AI)

What is the current special education teacher-to-pupil ratio, and what conditions must a school meet to set up special education classes?

Asked by Tuan Tan Kok Wai [Cheras]

A (AI)

The ideal ratio in a PPKI class is one teacher to seven pupils, but this cannot be achieved in Klang Valley, especially Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, where the ratio is very high. Conditions for setting up or expanding special education classes include demand, available space or facilities for the class, and availability of teaching staff, which is the most important and a major challenge. KPM is increasing the placement and training of special education teachers, and PISMP special education training intake was 115 in 2020, 180 in 2021, 90 in 2022, 100 in 2023, 194 in 2024 and 460 in 2025. KPM also offers short courses to identify mainstream teachers for PPKI.

Answered by Tuan Wong Kah Woh, Timbalan Menteri Pendidikan
