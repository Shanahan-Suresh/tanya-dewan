Q (AI)

What is the total durian export volume to China since August 2024, how is Malaysian durian received in China, and what are the main challenges to competitiveness?

Asked by Tuan Chow Yu Hui [Raub]

A (AI)

Market access was obtained on 19 June 2024 and the first shipment of 40 metric tonnes went by air on 24 August 2024. Department of Statistics data show 413.61 metric tonnes worth RM24.84 million were exported to China from 24 August to 31 December 2024. Malaysian durian is well received and sold in 16 Chinese cities and provinces. Main challenges are supply that cannot meet demand, short shelf life and higher logistics costs.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan [Datuk Arthur Joseph Kurup]

Figures given (11)
  • 19 Jun 2024 date market access was obtained
  • 40 metric tonnes in the first air shipment on 24 August 2024
  • 413.61 metric tonnes of fresh durian exported to China from 24 August to 31 December 2024
  • RM24.84 juta value of those exports
  • 16 number of Chinese provinces and cities where Malaysian durian is available
  • 95 orchards approved by GACC
  • 48 packaging facilities approved by GACC
  • RM32 bilion size of China's fresh durian market in 2023
  • dua kali setahun durian harvest seasons per year
  • tiga ke-empat hari days within which durian must be marketed after harvest
  • 25 days shelf life of durian from Thailand and Vietnam

Q (AI)

Based on comparative studies at this stage, what assurance is there that separating the roles of the Peguam Negara and the Pendakwa Raya will keep the Pendakwa Raya independent of the Peguam Negara?

Asked by Tuan Chow Yu Hui [Raub]

A (AI)

Comparative studies, such as in Australia, show safeguards: the Pendakwa Raya does not report to the Peguam Negara and generally no instructions may be given to the Pendakwa Raya. An exception applies in national security cases, where the Peguam Negara may give specific instructions in writing that are gazetted for public knowledge.

Answered by Tuan M. Kulasegaran a/l Murugeson, Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Undang-undang dan Reformasi Institusi)
