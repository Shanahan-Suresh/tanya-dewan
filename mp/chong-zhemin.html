Q (AI)

What is the government's strategy to attract new semiconductor and AI investment and keep Malaysia competitive in the region?

Asked by Tuan Chong Zhemin [Kampar]

A (AI)

The government launched the National Semiconductor Strategy in 2024 to move toward high-value activities, using three main strategies, the New Incentive Framework from 1 March 2026, and the Data Centre Task Force. Approved semiconductor investment from January 2024 to March 2026 reached RM91.9 billion, and 18,062 local talents had been trained by December 2025 against a target of 60,000.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri Pelaburan, Perdagangan dan Industri [Tuan Sim Tze Tzin]

Figures given (6)
  • RM91.9 bilion Approved semiconductor investment, January 2024 to March 2026
  • RM83 bilion Foreign direct investment (FDI) within that approved semiconductor investment
  • RM8.9 bilion Domestic direct investment (DDI) within that approved semiconductor investment
  • 60,000 Target number of trained skilled workers
  • 18,062 Local talents trained as of December 2025
  • 1 Mac 2026 Start date of the New Incentive Framework

  • USD60 Lower crude oil price scenario per barrel
  • USD80 Upper crude oil price scenario per barrel
  • RM5 bilion Savings from the earlier diesel subsidy rationalisation
