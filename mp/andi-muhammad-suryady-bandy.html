Q (AI)

What are gig workers' rights if the employer's contracting entity changes contract terms without their consent?

Asked by Datuk Andi Muhammad Suryady bin Bandy [Kalabakan]

A (AI)

Such a change is a dispute that breaches the rights in paragraph 8(1)(F) of the Gig Workers Act 2025, including changes to floor rates and commission rates. Workers should first use the company's internal grievance mechanism, then file a complaint with KESUMA via eAduan, and unresolved disputes go to the Gig Workers Tribunal.

Answered by Datuk Khairul Firdaus bin Akbar Khan, Timbalan Menteri Sumber Manusia

Figures given (3)
  • 8(1)(F) Paragraph of the Akta Pekerja Gig 2025 setting out the breached right
  • Akta 872 Act number of the Akta Pekerja Gig 2025
  • 2025 Year of the Akta Pekerja Gig
