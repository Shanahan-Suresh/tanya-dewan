Q (AI)

Has a comprehensive impact assessment been made of existing R&D fiscal incentives, and what policy exists to encourage multinationals to do substantive R&D in Malaysia?

Asked by Dato' Seri Ir. Dr. Ahmad Samsuri bin Mokhtar [Kemaman]

A (AI)

The government regularly assesses these incentives and cites results from Cradle Fund, MRANTI, MySTI and BIG, and says tax matters sit with MIDA, MITI and MOF, which approved double tax deductions for contributions to the Malaysia Science Endowment and extended the angel investor tax incentive, due to end in 2026, with MOSTI hoping for extension to 2030.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri Sains, Teknologi dan Inovasi [Dato' Dr. Mohammad Yusof bin Apdal]

Figures given (23)
  • 2003 year Cradle was established
  • 1,300 number of startups Cradle has funded
  • RM280 juta funding provided by Cradle to startups
  • RM1.6 bilion follow-on financing attracted
  • RM1.02 bilion contribution to GDP
  • 82,000 jobs created
  • 76.2 peratus prototype commercialisation rate
  • 2025 year of MRANTI value creation and initiatives
  • RM443.9 juta invention value generated in 2025
  • 113 products or technologies commercialised cumulatively
  • 798 local products approved with MySTI logo
  • Mei 2026 MySTI approval count date
  • RM70 juta government procurement value generated by MySTI products
  • Jun 2023 hingga Mei 2026 period of MySTI procurement value
  • lapan number of GLCs collaborating under BIG in 2025
  • 20 number of startups collaborating with GLCs in 2025
  • 2026 year BIG was extended to public-listed companies as BIG 2.0
  • enam target number of new companies under BIG 2.0
  • RM5,000 hingga RM500,000 range of qualifying investments under the angel investor tax incentive
  • 2018 year from which investments were approved under the incentive
  • RM3 juta investments approved under the incentive
  • 31 Disember 2026 end date of the angel investor tax incentive
  • 2030 proposed extension period of the incentive
