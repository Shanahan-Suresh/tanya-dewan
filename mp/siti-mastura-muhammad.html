How much federal land has been encroached on nationwide and in Penang, and what action and development plans apply?

Asked by Dr. Siti Mastura binti Muhammad [Kepala Batas]

A (AI)

There were 465 encroachments from 2015 to May 2025 on federal land totalling 215,406 hectares; Penang had 26 complaints covering 18 lots. What preventive and periodic monitoring measures are taken to stop federal land being encroached on again, especially in Penang?

Asked by Dr. Siti Mastura binti Muhammad [Kepala Batas]

A (AI)

  • RM500,000 maximum fine if an encroacher is found guilty in court
