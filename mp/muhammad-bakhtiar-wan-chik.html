Q (AI)

Asked by Dato' Sri Dr. Haji Ismail bin Haji Abd. Muttalib [Maran]

A (AI)

From 2014 to October 2019 the ministry acted on 2,035 tourism complaints, 391 of them on umrah or ziarah package businesses, and opened 612 investigation papers under Act 482. Ninety-one umrah or ziarah cases received compound offers, and in 2019 to October 350 complaints were received, 48 of them on umrah or ziarah packages. Seven cases received compound offers, three cases went to court, and 12 licences were cancelled for 2009 to 2017.
