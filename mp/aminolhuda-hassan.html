Asked by Tuan Haji Aminolhuda bin Hassan [Sri Gading]

A (AI)

The minimum wage rises from RM1,700 to RM2,000 and benefits over four million workers. Micro, small and medium enterprises with sales below RM50 million will be exempted to adjust.
