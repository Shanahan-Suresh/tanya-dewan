Does the government plan to make health checks mandatory for licence renewal from age 70, and how would this affect road safety and elderly mobility?

Asked by Tuan Mohd Nazri bin Abu Hassan [Merbok]

A (AI)

Health checks are not currently required for private vehicle licence renewal at age 70 and above. Police data show a small share of accidents involve those aged 70 and above, and MIROS found no conclusive evidence that age-based mandatory checks significantly reduce accidents. Q (AI)

How many inspections of merchant ships passing through the Strait of Malacca were carried out?

Asked by Datuk Haji Hasbi bin Haji Habibollah

A (AI)

No separate inspections were carried out; checks are done through Port State Control on the Ballast Water Management Record Book. The BWM Convention has been enforced in Malaysia since 8 September 2017 under MSN 07/2017. Ships must exchange or treat ballast water at more than 200 nautical miles from land and in water at least 200 metres deep, and most ships now have their own treatment plants.

Q (AI)

Does Malaysia apply the Polluter Pays Principle?

Asked by Datuk Haji Hasbi bin Haji Habibollah

A (AI)

Yes, through international liability and compensation regimes. The 1992 Civil Liability Convention applies to tankers carrying more than 2,000 tonnes of cargo, and the 1992 Fund Convention applies to all ships above 1,000 gross tonnage. Ship owners bear strict liability and must hold insurance, with additional compensation available from the IOPC Funds, and the JLM helps coordinate claims.

Answered by Datuk Haji Hasbi bin Haji Habibollah, Timbalan Menteri Pengangkutan

Figures given (2)
  • 2,000 tan Cargo threshold for tankers covered by the 1992 Civil Liability Convention
  • 1,000 Gross tonnage threshold for ships covered by the 1992 Fund Convention
