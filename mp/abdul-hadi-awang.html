Q (AI)

If that is illegal, is the Perikatan Nasional attempt to topple the government, said to be inspired by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, also illegal?

Asked by Tuan Sanisvara Nethaji Rayer a/l Rajaji [Jelutong]

A (AI)

Under parliamentary democracy a government can be changed by a no-confidence motion, by MPs’ support, or by dissolution and fresh election, which is not wrong; the Yang di-Pertuan Agong acts under the constitutional monarchy.

Answered by Tan Sri Dato’ Seri Haji Abdul Hadi bin Awang [Marang]

Q (AI)

What steps will the government take under Wawasan Kemakmuran Bersama to narrow the economic and social gaps between Bumiputera and non-Bumiputera?

Asked by Dato' Seri Haji Abdul Hadi Awang [Marang]

A (AI)

The government aims for shared prosperity, gives weight to equitability of outcomes alongside equality of opportunities, provides training and support for those not yet capable, and targets a decent standard of living for all Malaysians by 2030.

Answered by Perdana Menteri [Tun Dr. Mahathir bin Mohamad]

Figures given (9)
  • 20 tahun Length of the 20-year development programme known as the New Economic Policy
  • 1970-an Decade in which the New Economic Policy was launched
  • 2020 Target year of Wawasan 2020 for becoming a developed nation
  • hampir 50 tahun Nearly 50 years of implementing the DEB and Wawasan 2020
  • 2030 Year by which all Malaysians are to attain a decent standard of living
  • ke-5 dan ke-6 The fifth and sixth Prime Ministers, whose government policies the answer says failed to meet Wawasan 2020's original aims
