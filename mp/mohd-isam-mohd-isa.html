What is the current status of the study to draft a specific law on ticket scalping, what are the weaknesses of existing law, and when will the proposal be tabled?

Asked by Dato' Mohd Isam bin Mohd Isa [Tampin]

A (AI)

There is no specific provision yet. KPDN is engaging event organisers and benchmarking other countries, and is studying regulations under section 15(1) of Akta 723, covering definition, scope, bulk purchases and ID-linked tickets. Catut rules cannot apply to scalping because the event is one-off.

  • Akta 723 Act under which regulations may be made

  • tiga tahun Three years of sales data needed for catut calculation
