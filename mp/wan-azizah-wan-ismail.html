Questions answered

Q (AI)

Why has the ministry not raised the marriage age to 18 as Indonesia recently did?

Asked by Tuan Syed Ibrahim bin Syed Noh [Ledang]

A (AI)

Government has taken legal steps and consulted states on a uniform minimum age of 18, but Islamic family law is a state matter and only some states have agreed to amend; non-Muslim registration age is already 18 under Act 164 with an improved SOP for exemptions; Indonesia raised its female marriage age to 19 but still allows court-approved exceptions; a national strategic plan is being finalised.

Answered by Masyarakat [Dato’ Seri Dr. Wan Azizah Wan Ismail], Menteri Pembangunan Wanita, Keluarga dan Masyarakat

Figures given (9)
  • 18 tahun Minimum marriage age the government seeks to standardise, and the existing registration minimum age for non-Muslims
  • Lima Number of states that have agreed to amend the Islamic family law minimum age
  • tujuh Number of states that have not agreed to amend the law
  • 16 tahun dan 17 tahun Ages of girls who may marry with permission from the Chief Minister or Menteri Besar
  • 22 Ogos Date the improved SOP on marriage under age took effect
  • 1974 Year Indonesia first amended its marriage law
  • 16 tahun kepada 19 tahun Indonesia's increase in the minimum marriage age for women
  • 16 September 2019 Date the Indonesian DPR passed the amendment
  • 61 Number of agencies involved in the draft National Strategic Plan

Q (AI)

Why can child grooming, an offence under the Child Sexual Offences Act 2017, not be used to arrest people who groom children for marriage?

Asked by Tuan Syed Ibrahim bin Syed Noh [Ledang]

A (AI)

Act 792 can apply where sexual offence elements exist, especially where the offender is much older, but it was mainly drafted for pornography and online grooming; in child marriage cases such elements are often absent or unprovable, and parents or children may agree; the government is examining the Act's constraints with BHEUU.

Answered by Dato’ Seri Dr. Wan Azizah Wan Ismail, Menteri Pembangunan Wanita, Keluarga dan Masyarakat
