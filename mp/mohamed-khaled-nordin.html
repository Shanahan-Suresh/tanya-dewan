Yang Berhormat Tanah Merah asked about the helicopter lease procurement and the award of the Black Hawk lease contract to Aerotree Defence and Services Sdn. Bhd.

Asked by Dato’ Seri Mohamed Khaled bin Nordin

A (AI)

The procurement began in 2022, was advertised from 15 Julai 2022 to 11 Ogos 2022 and tabled to the Lembaga Perolehan in Oktober 2022. The minister noted that Yang Berhormat Tanah Merah was Deputy Minister of Defence at the time. The lease is for 28 helicopters over 18 years, covering supply, insurance, maintenance, repair and overhaul, training and out-of-base maintenance, with a minimum availability of 85 per cent. After 15 years the government may buy all 28 units for RM1, though the minister said each unit is estimated at RM30 juta to RM50 juta.
