How effective is coordination between KKM, KPM, KBS and KPKT under the national haze action plan in protecting at-risk groups, and will SOPs such as work from home, online PdP and alternative arrangements for SPM and STPM be introduced?

A (AI)

Q (AI)

What is the difference between the first and second ASEAN Haze-Free Roadmaps, and how are weaknesses addressed?

Asked by Dato’ Sri Tuan Ibrahim bin Tuan Man [Kubang Kerian]

A (AI)

The second roadmap adds a polluter pays principle, but the AATHP is cooperative and has no specific compensation-claim mechanism, so priority is strengthening it through diplomacy, information sharing and joint action. The vision is a haze-free ASEAN by 2030, with indicators such as fewer hotspots and burnt areas, fewer days at Alert Level 2 and 3, and no Alert Level 3 days for two consecutive years.

Answered by Dato’ Sri Arthur Joseph Kurup, Menteri Sumber Asli dan Kelestarian Alam

Figures given (4)
  • 2023–2030 Period of the second ASEAN Haze-Free Roadmap
  • 2030 Target year for a haze-free ASEAN
  • 2 dan 3 ASEAN Alert Levels whose number of days is to be reduced
  • dua Number of consecutive years with no Alert Level 3 days as a target
