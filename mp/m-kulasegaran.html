Questions answered

Q (AI)

How do the structure, assessment method and duration of the NBC differ from the CLP, and what is the pathway and transition mechanism for candidates who have not passed the CLP?

Asked by Dr. Sivakumar a/l Varatharaju Naidu [Batu Gajah]

A (AI)

The NBC replaces the CLP with a Conversion Course for non-qualifying law degree holders from overseas institutions and a LPPC; the CC lasts three months and the LPPC is proposed at six months. A NBC Taskforce set up on 27 April 2026 is studying transition mechanisms, including policies for candidates who have not passed the CLP, and the Board will consider them once the study is finished.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Undang-undang dan Reformasi Institusi) [Tuan M. Kulasegaran a/l V. Murugeson]

Figures given (6)
  • 27 April 2026 Date the Lembaga Kelayakan Profesion Undang-undang set up the NBC Taskforce
  • tiga bulan Duration of the Conversion Course (CC) programme
  • lima modul Number of modules candidates must study for the CC programme
  • enam bulan Proposed duration of the LPPC programme
  • lima modul Number of modules candidates must study for the LPPC programme
  • tiga komponen taksiran Number of assessment components for the LPPC programme

Q (AI)

Who is liable for compensation arising from freezing orders, and are agencies protected from legal action?

Asked by Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Undang-undang dan Reformasi Institusi) [Tuan M. Kulasegaran a/l V. Murugeson]

A (AI)

Under subsection 46(6) of Act 613, enforcement agencies are not liable for compensation or costs unless the order was not made in good faith; section 77 gives protection from legal action only if actions were taken in good faith and that protection is not absolute.

Answered by Timbalan Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri (Undang-undang dan Reformasi Institusi) [Tuan M. Kulasegaran a/l V. Murugeson]

Figures given (2)
  • 46(6) subsection of Act 613 on agency non-liability for compensation
  • 77 section of Act 613 giving agencies legal protection
