Q (AI)

Asked by Puan Young Syefura binti Othman [Bentong]

  • 22 public sector agencies whose services are integrated
  • 2027 year from which the reusable platform ecosystem approach begins
  • 2028 start of the period targeting integrated, people-centred services
  • 2030 end of the period targeting integrated, people-centred services

What is the status of the transfer of Dara, Amoi and Kelat to Tennoji Zoo, and what indicators are used to assess their physical and psychological welfare?

Asked by Puan Young Syefura binti Othman [Bentong]

The three Asian elephants were sent to Zoo Tennoji under an agreement between Zoo Taiping & Night Safari and Zoo Tennoji; PERHILITAN's evaluation found the zoo compliant with the 2012 regulations; welfare is assessed against physical criteria (health, diet, facilities) and psychological and behavioural indicators (natural behaviour, stereotypic behaviour, response to carers, stress hormones, environmental enrichment).
